# CalcQuest: the brain puzzle game, explained

CalcQuest is a free brain puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web, with 20+ top-rated puzzle games, 350+ handcrafted levels, a Journey map, a daily challenge and friend duels. It is built for a five-minute daily puzzle habit that leaves you sharper, in place of endless scrolling. CalcQuest is made by one person, Arin Agrawal, and is played by 25K+ people in 150+ countries, with a 4.9-star rating.

This repository is the official CalcQuest documentation. Read it on the web at **[calcquest.app/docs](https://calcquest.app/docs)**.

**Get the game:** [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.calcquest.calculatorgame) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6757161847) · [Play free in your browser](https://calcquest.app/puzzles)

## How you play

- **[The Journey](gameplay/journey.md)** is the main mode: a map of chapters where every level is a short run of different puzzles, with chests and trophies along the way.
- **[The daily challenge](gameplay/daily-challenge.md)** gives every player the same four puzzles each day, with a daily leaderboard.
- **[Friend duels](gameplay/duels.md)** let you challenge a friend to the same puzzle. The fastest time within 48 hours wins.
- **Timed and Endless** modes let you chase a high score or play with no clock at all. See [Game modes](gameplay/game-modes.md).
- **[Streaks and rewards](gameplay/progression-and-rewards.md):** XP, gems, daily streaks and real rewards through the [streak reward program](campaigns/streak-rewards.md).
- **[Play as a guest](gameplay/guest-play.md):** start in seconds with no account.

## Why CalcQuest is different

1. **Puzzles, not a feed.** A short daily ritual that challenges you, then lets you go.
2. **Handcrafted by a person.** 350+ levels designed by hand, and every puzzle has a logical solution.
3. **Ads never interrupt a puzzle.** No pop-up ads; rewarded videos are always optional. See [Monetization ethics](design-philosophy/monetization-ethics.md).
4. **Play with people you know.** Duels, friends, leaderboards and a shared daily challenge.
5. **Made by one developer.** Built and run by Arin Agrawal, with no paid advertising.
