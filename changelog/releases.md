# CalcQuest release history

CalcQuest was first released on Google Play in September 2024 as a calculator puzzle game and has been updated many times since. The current version is 2.2.3 (November 2026), which made CalcQuest a brain puzzle game built around the Journey, the daily challenge, friend duels, guest play, CalcQuest Premium and the streak reward program. This page summarises the significant releases, newest first.

## Version 2.2 (November 2026): CalcQuest becomes a brain puzzle game

Versions 2.2.1 to 2.2.3, released in November 2026.

- **New Journey.** A redesigned map of chapters, each level a run of mixed puzzle stages, with chests, trophies and a celebration for every chapter.
- **Friend duels.** Challenge a friend to the same board; the fastest time within 48 hours wins XP. A Duels tab, duel records and shareable duel links.
- **Guest-first play.** Start with just a name; sign in with phone, Google or Apple later, keeping your progress.
- **CalcQuest Premium.** Monthly, yearly and lifetime plans: no forced ads, every game and chapter, unlimited duels, 500 gems, early access and a badge.
- **Streak reward program.** Real rewards at 14, 30, 90, 180 and 365-day streaks.
- **Streaks.** A seven-day egg view, offline-safe streak days, and celebrations for streak milestones.
- **Timed and Endless** modes for every game, and a new game sheet with difficulty, stats and leaderboard.
- **Smarter reminders.** Reminders with daily and weekly caps and quiet hours, and a "beat your time" notification when a friend's daily time is beaten.
- **Links.** Game, duel, invite and profile links on calcquest.app that open the right screen, even after an install.
- **New Journey and Profile tabs**, a new design system, and full-screen effects for level-ups and rewards.

## Version 2.1 (2026): the puzzle collection grows

Versions 2.1.0 (December 2025) to 2.1.28 (October 2026).

- **September 2026 (2.1.27).** The first Journey: levels made of several mini-game stages, replacing the old world maps as the main mode. Daily challenge reminders.
- **July 2026.** Daily challenge limited to one attempt per day.
- **June 2026.** Shikaku and KenKen join. A leaderboard for the daily challenge.
- **May 2026.** The daily challenge launches. Google Play Games achievements. More puzzle games.
- **March 2026.** Number Alchemy. Google Play Games sign-in and leaderboards.
- **January 2026.** Launch on the App Store, with Sign in with Apple. Gems, in-app purchases through RevenueCat, and support for iPad.

## 2025: worlds, purchases and endless puzzles

- Generated puzzles and endless modes, so players never run out of content.
- The first mini-games beyond the calculator: math, memory and number games.
- Revives with optional rewarded ads in mini-games.
- Dark mode, worlds, Remote Config and in-app purchases.
- 250 more handcrafted calculator levels, taking the total past 350.

## September 2024: the first CalcQuest

- First release on Google Play on 3 September 2024.
- A calculator puzzle game: reach a target number with limited calculator presses.
- 135 handmade levels.
- A custom level editor followed in early 2025.
