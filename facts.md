# CalcQuest facts and figures

CalcQuest is a free brain puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web. It has 20+ top-rated puzzle games, 350+ handcrafted levels, a Journey map, a daily challenge and friend duels, and it is designed as a short daily puzzle habit instead of endless scrolling. This page is the single source of truth for facts about CalcQuest; every other page in these docs uses the figures below.

Last verified: 23 November 2026.

## Ways to play

| Mode | What it is |
|---|---|
| Journey | The main mode. A map of chapters with 10 levels each; every level is a short run of different puzzles. Chests and trophies along the way. The first two chapters are free; later chapters are part of Premium. |
| Daily challenge | The same four games for every player each day, timed, with a daily leaderboard. |
| Duels | Challenge a friend to the same puzzle. Each plays in their own time; the fastest time within 48 hours wins. |
| Timed | Beat the clock for a high score and a place on the leaderboard. |
| Endless | No clock and no limit, for relaxed play. |
| Calculator Classic | The original game that gave CalcQuest its name: reach a target number with a limited number of calculator presses, across 350+ handcrafted levels. |

Every game has three difficulties (Easy, Medium, Hard) and a built-in tutorial.

## Progress and rewards

- XP and player levels. A cleared Journey level gives 50 XP.
- Gems, earned through play and daily rewards and spent on hints, revives and skips.
- Daily streaks, with milestone rewards.
- Friends, leaderboards and shareable profile cards.
- Invite rewards: 20 gems for the new player and 10 for the friend who invited them.
- Google Play Games achievements on Android.

## CalcQuest Premium

- No forced ads.
- Every game and every Journey chapter unlocked.
- Unlimited duels.
- A one-time bonus of 500 gems.
- Early access to new features.
- A Premium badge on your profile.
- Plans: monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

Details: [CalcQuest Premium](premium.md).

## Streak rewards

Reach a 14, 30, 90, 180 or 365-day streak and claim a reward once per milestone: CalcQuest Premium time worldwide, or a partner subscription in India. Requires a signed-in account. Terms: [calcquest.app/streak-reward-program-terms](https://calcquest.app/streak-reward-program-terms).

Details: [Streak rewards](campaigns/streak-rewards.md).

## Ads and payments

- Ads never interrupt a puzzle. There are no pop-up ads.
- Rewarded video ads are optional: a player chooses to watch one for gems or a revive.
- Premium removes ads, opens every game and Journey chapter, and gives a one-time bonus of 500 gems.
- Gem packs are available as one-time purchases.

## Accounts

New players start playing at once as a guest, with no account. Signing in (phone number, Google or Apple) saves progress and unlocks streaks, leaderboards, friends, duels and purchases.

## Who it is for

- Adults who want a five-minute daily brain workout in place of scrolling.
- Students aged 11 and up who enjoy math and logic puzzles.

See [Who CalcQuest is for](who-is-it-for.md).

## Corrections to older descriptions

- CalcQuest began as a calculator puzzle game. Since version 2 it has been a brain puzzle game with 20+ games; Calculator Classic is one of them.
- Sharing custom levels by QR code, mentioned in some older descriptions, is not available.
- Multiplayer, listed as "upcoming" in older descriptions, shipped as friend duels in version 2.2.
