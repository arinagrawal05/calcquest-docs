# Monetization Ethics

This document describes CalcQuest's approach to monetization and the ethical principles guiding those decisions.

## Revenue Model

CalcQuest uses a **free-to-play with optional ad removal** model.

- The game is free to download and play.
- Advertisements are displayed at natural break points (e.g., between levels).
- Players can make a one-time in-app purchase to remove ads permanently.

## Principles

### No Pay-to-Win

Monetization does not affect gameplay balance. All levels are solvable without spending money. There are no purchasable hints, skips, or power-ups that trivialize challenges.

### No Deceptive Patterns

The game avoids dark patterns designed to manipulate spending:

- No artificial scarcity or urgency.
- No "accidental purchase" UI layouts.
- No currency obfuscation (e.g., buying gems that buy other things).

### Respect for Attention

Ads are placed at natural stopping points rather than interrupting active gameplay. The goal is to minimize frustration while acknowledging that ads enable free access.

### Clear Value Exchange

The ad removal purchase is straightforward:

- One payment removes all ads permanently.
- No recurring subscriptions required.
- No "premium tiers" that gate content.

### Family-Friendly Approach

Given the game's appeal to younger audiences, monetization avoids:

- Pressure tactics targeting children.
- Gambling-like mechanics (loot boxes, random rewards for payment).
- In-app purchases that require parental oversight to avoid unintended spending.

## Transparency

Players are informed about what they are purchasing before any transaction. The app store listings and in-game prompts clearly describe the ad removal feature.

## Future Considerations

As CalcQuest evolves, any additional monetization (such as cosmetic purchases) will be evaluated against these principles before implementation.
