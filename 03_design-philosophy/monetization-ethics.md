# Monetization Ethics

This document describes CalcQuest's approach to monetization and the ethical principles guiding those decisions.

## Our Philosophy: Respect First, Revenue Second

CalcQuest was built on a simple promise: **No player should ever see an ad they didn't choose to watch.** In an industry notorious for intrusive monetization, we believe ethical game design and sustainable revenue can coexist.

---

## Revenue Model

CalcQuest uses a **100% voluntary monetization** model with two revenue streams:

### 1. Opt-In Rewarded Ads
- **Zero forced ads**: We never interrupt your gameplay with advertisements
- **Your choice, your reward**: Watch an ad only when YOU want gems or rewards
- **Fair exchange**: Every ad watched = tangible in-game benefit (gems)
- **Natural timing**: Ad opportunities appear at break points, never mid-puzzle

### 2. In-App Purchases (IAP)
CalcQuest offers optional **gem** purchases for players who prefer direct support:

- **Hints**: Use gems to get nudges on challenging levels (preserves satisfaction of solving)
- **Revivals**: Continue endless mode runs without restarting (competitive edge for high scores)
- **Future: Exclusive Puzzles** *(Coming Soon)*: Premium handcrafted puzzle packs (NOT pay-to-win—Adventure Mode stays 100% free)

**What gems ARE NOT:**
- Not required to complete any level
- Not a "speed up waiting" mechanic (we don't have energy systems)
- Not randomized rewards (no loot boxes, no gambling mechanics)

---

## Core Ethical Principles

### 1. No Forced Interruptions
**Industry Standard**: Show ads every 3-5 actions, interrupt gameplay  
**CalcQuest**: You control when ads appear, or skip them entirely  

**Why this matters**: Respects your time and attention. Math puzzles require focus—ads should never break your flow state.

---

### 2. No Pay-to-Win
Every single one of our **350+ handcrafted levels** is solvable with zero spending. Gems provide:
- **Hints** (optional nudges, not solutions)
- **Endless mode revivals** (competitive feature, not story-gated)

**What you'll NEVER see:**
- Levels locked behind paywalls
- "Premium-only" difficulty settings
- Content that requires payment to access

---

### 3. No Deceptive Patterns (Dark Patterns)
We explicitly reject manipulative design:

❌ **No artificial scarcity**: "Only 3 gems left at this price!"  
❌ **No accidental purchases**: All IAP require confirmation  
❌ **No currency obfuscation**: Gems have clear, transparent value  
❌ **No bait-and-switch**: What you see in screenshots is what you play  
❌ **No fake urgency**: "Limited time offer expires in 5 minutes!"  

✅ **What we do instead**: Clear pricing, honest descriptions, straightforward exchanges

---

### 4. Family-Friendly & Child-Safe
With a core audience of **ages 11-26**, we take extra precautions:

- **No gambling mechanics**: Zero loot boxes, gacha, or random reward systems
- **No pressure tactics**: No popups asking "Are you sure you don't want gems?"
- **Parental peace of mind**: IAP are clearly marked and require device-level authorization
- **Educational integrity**: Monetization never compromises learning value

**Real-world consideration**: A 12-year-old can play CalcQuest without parents worrying about unexpected charges or manipulative upsells.

---

### 5. Transparent Value Exchange
Before any transaction (ad or purchase), you know exactly what you're getting:

- **Ad prompt**: "Watch a 30-second ad for 50 gems?"  
- **IAP screen**: "500 gems for $2.99 — use for hints or endless revivals"  
- **No hidden costs**: No subscriptions that auto-renew  
- **No bait pricing**: First purchase isn't cheaper to hook you

---

### 6. Sustainable Independence
As a **solo indie developer** (Arin Agrawal), ethical monetization isn't just moral—it's survival:

- **Why ads matter**: Enables 100% free gameplay for students/families who can't pay
- **Why IAP matters**: Supports ongoing development (multiplayer mode, new puzzles, iOS optimization)
- **Why opt-in works**: Players who love CalcQuest support it willingly; those who can't still play everything

**Transparency note**: Arin doesn't have VC funding or a corporate safety net. Your gem purchases and voluntary ad views directly fund CalcQuest's future.

## What This Looks Like in Practice

**Maya (14 years old, plays free)**

- Occasionally watches ads to earn gems for hints on tough puzzles
- Never feels pressured to spend money

**Raj (22 years old, buys gems)**

- Purchases 1000 gems for $4.99

**Ananya (17 years old, zero ads/IAP)**

## Industry Comparison

| Practice | Typical Mobile Games | CalcQuest |
|----------|---------------------|-----------|
| **Forced ads** | Every 2-5 minutes | Never—opt-in only |
| **Energy systems** | Wait 4 hours or pay | None—play unlimited |

## Future Monetization (Planned)

### Exclusive Puzzle Packs *(Not Yet Implemented)*
- **What**: Premium handcrafted puzzle collections (20-50 levels per pack)
- **Cost**: $1.99-$3.99 per pack
- **Why ethical**: 
  - Adventure Mode (350+ levels) stays free forever
  - Exclusive content is **additional**, not **withheld**
  - Each pack is a standalone purchase (no subscription trap)
  - Clear preview of what you're buying

**Guiding question**: "Would I feel good explaining this to a parent?"

---

## Commitment to Evolution

As CalcQuest grows, monetization may evolve—but **these principles will not**:

1. **No forced ads**—ever
2. **No pay-to-win mechanics**—core gameplay stays free
3. **No dark patterns**—transparent, honest design
4. **No exploiting young players**—family-safe by default

If we ever consider changes (cosmetics, battle pass, etc.), we'll:
- Test against these principles first
- Communicate changes in [changelog](../07_changelog/releases.md)
- Listen to community feedback via app reviews and social (@ArinBuilds)
