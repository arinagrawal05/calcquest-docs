# UX Decisions

This document explains key user experience decisions in CalcQuest and the reasoning behind them.

## Calculator Interface

### Decision

Use a simulated calculator as the primary interaction surface.

### Rationale

- The calculator is a universally recognized interface. Players immediately understand the input model.
- It reinforces the connection to arithmetic without feeling like a worksheet.
- The constrained interface simplifies design and focuses player attention.

## Level-Based Progression

### Decision

Organize content into discrete levels grouped by worlds.

### Rationale

- Provides clear milestones and sense of accomplishment.
- Allows difficulty to be curated per level.
- Enables players to return to specific challenges.

## Move Limits Over Time Limits

### Decision

Most campaign puzzles use move limits rather than time pressure.

### Rationale

- Reduces anxiety and allows thoughtful play.
- Emphasizes strategic planning over speed.
- More accessible to younger players and casual audiences.

Time-based modes exist as optional challenges for players who prefer them.

## Minimal Onboarding

### Decision

Keep tutorials short and integrated into early gameplay.

### Rationale

- Players learn by doing, not by reading walls of text.
- Respects returning players who already understand the basics.
- Reduces friction to starting the game.

## Simple Sharing Mechanisms

### Decision

Enable level sharing through QR codes and short text codes.

### Rationale

- QR codes are easily scanned and shared visually (social media, message apps).
- Text codes work in any context and are easily copied.
- No account creation required to share or receive levels.

## Optional Account System

### Decision

Core gameplay does not require account creation.

### Rationale

- Reduces barriers to play.
- Respects player privacy.
- Sign-in is available for those who want cloud sync and leaderboards.

## Consistent Visual Language

### Decision

Maintain a cohesive visual style across all screens and modes.

### Rationale

- Reinforces brand identity.
- Reduces cognitive load when navigating between features.
- Creates a polished, professional impression.
