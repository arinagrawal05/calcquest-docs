**Easy to learn, hard to master.** 
In CalcQuest, difficulty isn't a setting—it's a journey. From gentle introductions in World 1 to the logic-melting challenges of late-game campaign and survival modes, here is how we scale the "math-mountain."

### World-Based Scaling

Difficulty in campaign mode is organized by **worlds**. Each world introduces new concepts or constraints that increase complexity.

Early worlds focus on:
- Basic operations (addition, subtraction)
- Simple target numbers
- Generous move limits

Later worlds introduce:
- Additional operations (multiplication, division, special modifiers)
- Larger numbers or negative numbers
- Stricter move limits
- Multi-step solutions requiring planning

### Level-Within-World Scaling

Within each world, levels generally progress from simpler puzzles to more complex ones. This allows players to learn new mechanics before being tested on them.

## Endless Mode Scaling

Endless modes use **dynamic difficulty** that increases as the session progresses.

### Scaling Factors

- **Puzzle Complexity**: Solutions require more steps or less obvious operation sequences.
- **Number Range**: Target numbers and starting values may increase.
- **Time Pressure**: In timed modes, time limits may tighten.
- **Move Limits**: Available moves may decrease.

### Purpose

Dynamic scaling ensures that endless modes remain challenging for experienced players while providing an entry point for newcomers.

## Accessibility Considerations

CalcQuest aims to be approachable for players of varying skill levels:

- Early campaign content is accessible to players with basic arithmetic knowledge.
- No time pressure exists in standard campaign levels (unless explicitly designed as a challenge variant).
- Players can retry levels without penalty.
- Custom levels allow players to create content at their preferred difficulty.

## No Difficulty Selection

CalcQuest does not include an explicit difficulty selector (e.g., Easy/Medium/Hard). Difficulty is embedded in the content structure itself in campaign mode - players who seek challenge progress to later worlds or endless modes.
