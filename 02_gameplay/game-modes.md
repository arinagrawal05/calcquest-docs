# Game Modes

**How do you want to play today?**
CalcQuest offers three ways to challenge your brain: the curated path of Adventure Mode, the infinite challenge of Endless Mode, and the community-driven creativity of Custom Levels.

## Campaign Mode

The primary progression system in CalcQuest. Players advance through a series of **worlds**, each containing multiple **levels**.

### Structure

- Worlds are unlocked sequentially as players complete levels.
- Each world introduces new mechanics, operations, or constraints.
- Levels within a world generally increase in difficulty.

### Progression

- Completing a level awards experience points (XP).
- Accumulated XP contributes to player rank and leaderboard position.
- Some worlds or features may require reaching certain milestones to unlock.

## Endless Modes

Procedurally generated puzzle sequences that continue indefinitely until the player fails. CalcQuest includes 10+ distinct endless mode variants.

### Characteristics

- Puzzles are generated algorithmically, providing infinite variety.
- Difficulty typically increases as the session progresses.
- Sessions end when the player fails to solve a puzzle within constraints (time, moves, or other rules).
- Scores are tracked for high-score competition.

## Custom Levels

Players can create campaign levels using the in-game level editor. Created levels can be:

- Played locally
- Shared via **QR codes** (scannable images)
- Shared via **share codes** 


Other players can import and play these custom levels, extending the game's content beyond the handcrafted campaign.
