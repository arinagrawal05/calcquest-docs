**More than just addition.** 
From missing symbols to magic triangles and strategy-based number grids, CalcQuest features a rich variety of puzzle types that test your logic from every angle. 

## Core Mechanics

All puzzles share a common foundation:

1. A **current value** displayed on the calculator.
2. A **target value** the player must reach.
3. A set of **available operations** to apply.
4. Constraints such as **move limits**.

## Standard Puzzles (Campaign Mode)

These are the puzzle types used in the curated campaign levels. Campaign levels consist solely of these standard puzzles and do **not** mix with endless‑mode or procedural puzzles.

- **Missing Symbol Puzzles** – Players guess the missing math symbol to complete an equation.
- **Square Root Challenges** – Cracking clever square‑root problems.
- **Number Pair Matching** – Matching number pairs that hide secret logic.
- **Magic Triangle Puzzles** – Decoding the mystery of magic triangles.
- **Mental Arithmetic Levels** – Solving math problems directly in the head.
- **Quick Calculation Challenges** – Timed puzzles requiring racing against the clock.
- **Strategy‑Based Number Grids** – Unlocking and solving number grids through strategic approaches.
- **Picture‑Based Math Clues** – Finding math clues hidden inside pictures.
- **Number Pyramids** – Climbing through layers of number pyramids.
- **True or False Logic Checks** – Testing accuracy through true/false logic problems.
- **Multiple Choice Puzzles** – Hunting for the one correct answer among tricky options.

## Endless Puzzles (Procedural / Endless Modes)

In endless modes, puzzles are generated algorithmically and can reuse the same categories above, but they are presented without a fixed level structure, allowing infinite play.

### Types of Endless Puzzles

- **Operation‑Locked Endless Puzzles** – Limited operation sets.
- **Multi‑Step Endless Puzzles** – Require longer sequences of operations.
- **Negative Number Endless Puzzles** – Involve working with negative values.
- **Special Operation Endless Puzzles** – Include reverse, delete, append, replace.
- **Constraint Endless Puzzles** – Additional constraints like no specific digit, stay positive, exact moves.

These endless puzzles provide continuous challenges beyond the curated campaign experience.
