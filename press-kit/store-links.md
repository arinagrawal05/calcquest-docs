# CalcQuest store links

CalcQuest is free on Google Play for Android and on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and nine of its puzzles play in any browser at calcquest.app. These are the official links and identifiers to use in articles, directories and posts.

## Download links

| Store | Link | Identifier |
|---|---|---|
| Google Play (Android) | [play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.calcquest.calculatorgame](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.calcquest.calculatorgame) | Package `com.calcquest.calculatorgame` |
| App Store (iPhone, iPad) | [apps.apple.com/app/id6757161847](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6757161847) | App ID `6757161847` |
| One link for any phone | [calcquest.app/play](https://calcquest.app/play) | Sends Android to Google Play and iPhone to the App Store |

The package name `com.calcquest.calculatorgame` dates from CalcQuest's start as a calculator puzzle game and cannot be changed; it is the same app.
