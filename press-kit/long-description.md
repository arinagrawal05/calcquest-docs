## CalcQuest: handcrafted puzzles for brains tired of feeds

CalcQuest is a free brain puzzle game with 20+ top-rated puzzle games and 350+ handcrafted levels. It is built for a five-minute daily puzzle habit that leaves you sharper, in place of endless scrolling.

**20+ puzzle games in one app.** Logic puzzles such as Queens, Tango, Binairo and Minesweeper. Grid puzzles such as Mini Sudoku, Shikaku, Nonogram and Number Pyramid. Math puzzles such as KenKen, Math Grid and Quick Calculation. Memory games such as Flashback and Simon Sequence. And Calculator Classic, the original "reach the target" calculator puzzle that gave CalcQuest its name, with 350+ handcrafted levels.

**The Journey.** Travel a map of chapters where every level is a short run of different puzzles. Earn XP and gems, open chests and win a trophy for every chapter.

**A daily challenge.** Four puzzles, the same for every player that day. Set your best time, climb the daily leaderboard and see when you beat a friend.

**Friend duels.** Solve a board, then challenge a friend to the exact same one. They play in their own time; the fastest time wins.

**Streaks that pay off.** Play every day to grow your streak. At 14, 30, 90, 180 and 365 days, claim real rewards, from months of CalcQuest Premium to Premium for life.

**Start in seconds.** No account needed: type a name and play. Sign in later to save your progress and play with friends.

**Fair and calm.** Ads never interrupt a puzzle, and there are no pop-up ads, energy timers or loot boxes. Every game has Easy, Medium and Hard, and a tutorial that teaches by playing.

**CalcQuest Premium.** Go further with no forced ads, every game and Journey chapter unlocked, unlimited duels, 500 bonus gems, early access and a Premium badge. Monthly, yearly or lifetime.

**Made by one person.** CalcQuest is designed and built by Arin Agrawal, a solo developer in India. It is played by 25K+ people in 150+ countries, with a 4.9-star rating, and has grown with no paid advertising.

Download CalcQuest free on Google Play and the App Store, or play nine puzzles in your browser at calcquest.app.
