# CalcQuest brand guidelines

How to write about and show CalcQuest correctly. The name is always written **CalcQuest**: one word, capital C and capital Q. Describe it as a **brain puzzle game** with **20+ top-rated puzzle games** and **350+ handcrafted levels**. The brand colour is CalcQuest Yellow, #FACC15, and the logo with the mascot should never be stretched, recoloured or altered.

## The name

| Correct | Incorrect |
|---|---|
| CalcQuest | Calcquest, Calc Quest, CALCQUEST, Calc-Quest |
| CalcQuest Premium | CalcQuest Pro, CalcQuest Plus |

## How to describe CalcQuest

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Brain puzzle game | Calculator app, math drill app, math puzzle app |
| 20+ top-rated puzzle games | Exact counts that will change |
| 350+ handcrafted levels | "Procedurally generated" as the headline |
| Made by one developer, Arin Agrawal | "A studio", "a team" |
| Ads never interrupt a puzzle | "No ads at all" |
| 25K+ players in 150+ countries, 4.9 stars | Older figures such as 10K or 30,000 |

Do not describe CalcQuest as "the world's first" anything, and do not mention sharing custom levels by QR code, which is not available.

## Colour

| Name | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| CalcQuest Yellow | #FACC15 | The primary brand colour: highlights, buttons, accents |
| Ink | #1E1B18 | Text on yellow |

## Logo and mascot

- Use the official logo with its mascot, from [calcquest.app/press](https://calcquest.app/press).
- Do not stretch, crop, recolour, rotate or add effects to it.
- Give the logo room; do not crowd it with other marks or text.

## Store names and badges

Use the official "Get it on Google Play" and "Download on the App Store" badges from Google and Apple, linked to the [store links](store-links.md).

## Writing style

- Plain, warm and direct. Short sentences.
- No emoji in descriptions, documentation or press copy.
- Use real numbers only from [Facts and figures](../facts.md).
