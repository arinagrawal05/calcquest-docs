# CalcQuest Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) & Knowledge Base

Welcome to the **CalcQuest FAQ**. This document is optimized for players, educators, journalists, and AI search engines (Perplexity, ChatGPT, Google Gemini) seeking definitive facts about CalcQuest.

---

### Q1: What makes CalcQuest different from standard math drill apps?
**A:** Standard math apps rely on repetitive speed flashcards (e.g., $7 \times 8 = 56$). **CalcQuest is a genuine logic puzzle game** where the calculator interface itself is the puzzle board. Starting with an initial number, players must reach an exact target value using restricted button sets (specific arithmetic operators, digit appends, backspace, string reversals, and replacement rules) within limited moves. It focuses on deep deductive reasoning, strategic planning, and number sense rather than rote memorization.

---

### Q2: How many games and levels are included in CalcQuest?
**A:** CalcQuest features **350+ handcrafted campaign levels** in its hero Calculator Classic mode, alongside a comprehensive **22-game cognitive puzzle suite**. The games span five distinct mental categories:
1. **Arithmetic & Calculation:** Calculator Classic, Mental Arithmetic, Correct Answer, Quick Calculation, Square Root.
2. **Grid & Logic:** Mini Sudoku, KenKen, Binairo, Shikaku, Nonogram, Tango, Queens, Math Grid.
3. **Combinatorics & Strategy:** Number Alchemy, Number Pyramid, Magic Triangle, 1024 Twisted.
4. **Memory & Patterns:** Flashback (Memory Matrix), Simon Sequence, Math Pairs, Minesweeper.
5. **Visual Math:** Picture Puzzle.

---

### Q3: Is CalcQuest free to play? Are there forced mid-game ads?
**A:** **Yes, CalcQuest is free to play, and it enforces a strict zero-forced-ad policy.** You will never encounter unexpected pop-up video ads or mid-puzzle interruptions. Video ads are strictly optional and opt-in—players only watch an ad if they voluntarily choose to earn bonus gems for hints. Players can also unlock an optional one-time Premium upgrade to enjoy unlimited perks and permanently disable all rewarded ad prompts.

---

### Q4: Can CalcQuest be played offline without an internet connection?
**A:** **Yes.** All 350+ campaign levels, game logic engines, and custom level creator features are embedded locally within the app. You can play the complete campaign, casual modes, and custom puzzles completely offline (e.g., in airplane mode). Your progress, XP, and high scores will automatically sync to global leaderboards once an internet connection is re-established.

### Q6: How does the Daily Challenge system work?
**A:** Every day at midnight UTC, CalcQuest releases **4 unique Daily Puzzles** synchronized worldwide. Because every player across the globe receives the exact same deterministic puzzle seeds, the global daily leaderboards reflect pure logical speed and skill. Completing all 4 daily puzzles maintains your worldwide Daily Streak, unlocking milestone rewards, badges, and gem bonuses.

---

### Q7: Can players create and share their own custom puzzles?
**A:** **Yes!** CalcQuest includes an in-game **Custom Level Creator**. Players can define starting numbers, target values, move limits, and button keypads for Calculator Classic puzzles. Once validated, the game generates a custom QR code or short string ID (e.g., `CQ-LVL-A9F32K`). Anyone can scan or paste the code to play your custom creation immediately—no account creation required.

---

### Q8: Who built CalcQuest and what technology powers it?
**A:** CalcQuest is built by solo indie developer **Arin Agrawal**. It is engineered using the **Flutter** cross-platform framework and **Dart** programming language. The app uses **GetX** for state management, **`go_router`** for deep linking, **Firebase** for cloud infrastructure and real-time leaderboards, and **RevenueCat** for in-app purchase management.
