# CalcQuest FAQ

Answers to the most common questions about CalcQuest, the free brain puzzle game with 20+ top-rated puzzle games, 350+ handcrafted levels, a Journey map, a daily challenge and friend duels. Each answer is short and complete; follow the links for detail.

## About the game

### What is CalcQuest?

CalcQuest is a free brain puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web. It has 20+ top-rated puzzle games, such as Queens, Tango, KenKen, Mini Sudoku, Nonogram and Minesweeper, plus 350+ handcrafted levels, a Journey map, a daily challenge and friend duels. It is built for a five-minute daily puzzle habit in place of scrolling.

### Is CalcQuest a math game or a brain puzzle game?

CalcQuest is a brain puzzle game. It includes math puzzles such as KenKen and Calculator Classic, but also logic puzzles (Queens, Tango, Binairo, Minesweeper), grid puzzles (Mini Sudoku, Shikaku, Nonogram) and memory games (Flashback, Simon Sequence).

### Why is it called CalcQuest?

CalcQuest began in 2024 as a calculator puzzle game, where you reach a target number with a limited number of calculator presses. That game is still in the app as Calculator Classic, and the name stayed as CalcQuest grew into a collection of 20+ puzzle games.

### How many puzzle games and levels does CalcQuest have?

CalcQuest has 20+ top-rated puzzle games in four categories (Logic, Puzzle, Math and Memory) and 350+ handcrafted levels. Most games also generate fresh boards without limit at three difficulties, so you never run out of puzzles.

### What puzzle games are in CalcQuest?

Logic: Queens, Tango, Binairo, Minesweeper, Number Alchemy. Puzzle: Mini Sudoku, Shikaku, Nonogram, Number Pyramid, Picture Puzzle, 1024 Twisted. Math: Calculator Classic, Calculator, KenKen, Math Grid, Quick Calculation, Math Pairs, Correct Answer. Memory: Flashback, Simon Sequence, Magic Triangle, Rootdown, Mental Arithmetic.

### Who makes CalcQuest?

CalcQuest is made by one person, Arin Agrawal, a developer from Madhya Pradesh, India. He designs, builds and runs the whole game, and released the first version in September 2024.

### How many people play CalcQuest?

CalcQuest has 25K+ players in 150+ countries and a 4.9-star rating. It has grown through word of mouth, with no paid advertising.

## Playing

### What is the Journey in CalcQuest?

The Journey is CalcQuest's main mode: a map of chapters with 10 levels each. Every level is a short run of different puzzles, and clearing it moves you along the map and earns XP and gems. Chests and trophies mark the way. The first two chapters are free; later chapters are part of Premium.

### What is the CalcQuest daily challenge?

The daily challenge is four puzzle games, the same for every player that day. Your total time goes on the daily leaderboard, and friends get a nudge when you beat their time. You get one attempt per day.

### How do duels work in CalcQuest?

You play a puzzle, then challenge a friend to the exact same board. Your friend plays in their own time. The fastest time wins, and the duel ends after 48 hours or as soon as both of you have played. The winner earns XP, and wins and win streaks show on your profile.

### Can I play CalcQuest with friends?

Yes. You can add friends, send them duels, compare times on the daily challenge and per-game leaderboards, and share your profile card. Inviting a friend gives you 10 gems and them 20.

### Do I need an account to play?

No. You can start at once as a guest, with only a name. Sign in with your phone number, Google or Apple when you want to save progress across devices, keep a streak, add friends, play duels or buy Premium.

### Can I play CalcQuest offline?

Yes, the puzzle games play offline. Features that involve other people or your account, such as duels, leaderboards, the daily leaderboard and cloud saving, need an internet connection.

### Can I play CalcQuest in a browser?

Yes. Nine puzzles play free in a browser at calcquest.app: Queens, Tango, KenKen, Mini Sudoku, Shikaku, Nonogram, Binairo, Minesweeper and Number Pyramid. The full game, with the Journey, daily challenge and duels, is in the Android and iOS app.

### How hard are the puzzles?

Every game has Easy, Medium and Hard. Inside a session, difficulty also rises every few rounds. Each game starts with a short tutorial that teaches the rules by playing.

### Does every puzzle have a solution?

Yes. The 350+ handcrafted levels were each designed and tested by hand, and logic puzzles such as Queens are generated so that a board can be solved by reasoning.

## Streaks, rewards and Premium

### How do streaks work in CalcQuest?

A day counts toward your streak when you play: finish a game or a Journey level. The streak screen shows your last seven days, and a streak resets if you miss a day. Streaks need a signed-in account.

### What is the CalcQuest streak reward program?

When your streak reaches 14, 30, 90, 180 or 365 days, you can claim a reward once per milestone: CalcQuest Premium time for players everywhere, or, in India, a partner subscription such as JioHotstar, Spotify or YouTube Premium. See the streak rewards page for the full ladder and terms.

### What is CalcQuest Premium?

CalcQuest Premium is the optional paid plan. It removes forced ads, unlocks every game and Journey chapter, gives unlimited duels, a one-time bonus of 500 gems, early access to new features and a Premium badge. It comes as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase.

### Is CalcQuest free?

Yes. CalcQuest is free to download and play. Premium and gem packs are optional, and the daily challenge, streaks, friends and the first two Journey chapters are free for everyone.

### Does CalcQuest have ads?

Ads never interrupt a puzzle, and there are no pop-up ads. You can choose to watch a rewarded video for gems or a revive. Premium removes forced ads.

### What are gems used for?

Gems are CalcQuest's in-game currency. You earn them by playing, from daily rewards, XP milestones, invites and optional rewarded videos, and you spend them on hints, revives, skipping a Journey stage and avatars.

## Safety and support

### Is CalcQuest suitable for kids?

CalcQuest suits players aged 11 and up. It has no loot boxes or gambling mechanics, ads never interrupt a puzzle, purchases go through the store's own confirmation, and children can play as a guest without sharing personal details.

### Can teachers use CalcQuest in class?

Yes. The math and logic games work as warm-ups or rewards, and nine puzzles play in a browser with no install. For school programmes see calcquest.app/education.

### Which devices does CalcQuest run on?

Android phones and tablets through Google Play, iPhones and iPads through the App Store, and any modern browser for the nine web puzzles.

### How do I report a bug or get help?

Use the support form at calcquest.app/support, email arin@calcquest.app, or message @ArinBuilds on X. Every report is read by the developer.

### Can I share custom levels by QR code?

No. Some older descriptions of CalcQuest mention QR-code level sharing, but it is not available in the app.
