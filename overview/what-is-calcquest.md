# What is CalcQuest?

CalcQuest is a free brain puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web. It brings 20+ top-rated puzzle games, among them Queens, Tango, KenKen, Mini Sudoku, Nonogram and Minesweeper, together with 350+ handcrafted levels, a Journey map, a daily challenge and friend duels. It is designed for a five-minute daily puzzle habit that leaves you sharper, in place of endless scrolling.

## The idea in one line

Handcrafted puzzles for brains tired of feeds.

Most apps on a phone are built to keep you scrolling. CalcQuest is built to challenge you for a few minutes and then let you go, and to make you want to come back tomorrow.

## What you do in CalcQuest

1. **Play the Journey.** Move along a map of chapters. Each level is a short run of different puzzles, so one level might ask for a Queens board, a quick sum and a memory pattern. Chests and trophies mark the way. See [The Journey](../gameplay/journey.md).
2. **Take the daily challenge.** Four puzzles, the same for every player that day, timed, with a leaderboard. See [Daily challenge](../gameplay/daily-challenge.md).
3. **Duel a friend.** Solve a board, then send the same board to a friend. The fastest time wins. See [Friend duels](../gameplay/duels.md).
4. **Play any game on its own.** Pick from 20+ games in Timed mode for a high score or Endless mode with no clock. See [All puzzle games](../games/index.md).
5. **Keep your streak.** Play every day to grow a streak, earn XP and gems, and claim real rewards at streak milestones. See [Progress and rewards](../gameplay/progression-and-rewards.md).

## Where the name comes from

CalcQuest started in September 2024 as a calculator puzzle game: reach a target number using a calculator with a limited number of button presses. That game lives on as [Calculator Classic](../games/calculator-classic.md), with its 350+ handcrafted levels. Players asked for more variety, and CalcQuest grew into a full brain puzzle game. The story is in [From calculator game to brain puzzles](../dev-journey/from-calculator-game-to-brain-puzzles.md).

## What makes it different

- **Handcrafted by a person.** CalcQuest is designed, built and run by one developer, [Arin Agrawal](founder.md). The 350+ levels of Calculator Classic were each designed by hand, and the generated puzzles are tuned so every board has a logical solution.
- **Ads never interrupt a puzzle.** There are no pop-up ads. Rewarded videos are optional, and [Premium](../premium.md) removes ads.
- **Play first, sign up later.** You can start in seconds as a guest. See [Play as a guest](../gameplay/guest-play.md).
- **Social without being a social network.** Friends, duels, a shared daily challenge and leaderboards, with no feed.
