# Arin Agrawal, founder of CalcQuest

Arin Agrawal is the founder and only developer of CalcQuest. He is a Flutter developer from Madhya Pradesh, India, who built the first version of CalcQuest in two weeks during his first semester of college and released it on Google Play in September 2024. He designs, builds and runs the game alone, and grew it to 25K+ players in 150+ countries with no paid advertising.

## What he does

Arin handles every part of CalcQuest: product decisions, game and level design, the Flutter app for Android and iOS, the backend on Firebase, the website at calcquest.app, the store listings and the marketing. Before CalcQuest he built and published several other cross-platform apps.

He also placed seventh in a national hackathon.

## What drives the work

Arin is inspired by independent developers and by how Duolingo turned a daily habit into something people enjoy. His aim for CalcQuest is a puzzle game that feels premium every day and stays free to play. In the longer run, he wants to build products that make learning engaging and accessible worldwide.

He builds in public on X, sharing what worked, what did not and the numbers behind it.
