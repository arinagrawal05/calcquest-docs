# CalcQuest real-world moments

This is a running log of real moments that CalcQuest has created outside the screen: families playing together, strangers becoming players, and feedback that changed the game. New moments are added at the top; old ones are never edited.

## October 2025: a family in Germany

**What happened.** Arin replied on X to Melanie ([@MSiekmoeller](https://x.com/MSiekmoeller)), a Flutter developer in Germany who had just reached 400 followers. She looked at his profile, found CalcQuest on Google Play and installed it.

A few minutes later she sent a message:

> "Very cool game! One thing: when playing for the first time, the goal/moves aren't very self-explaining. Maybe add a small onboarding? I'll show it to my children and compete with them."

Later that day she replied to Arin's post about reaching 5,000 installs:

> "My whole family is playing this game right now. It's great for in-between times."

([Her post on X, 24 October 2025](https://x.com/MSiekmoeller/status/1981704468631810390))

Another developer joined the thread to say they would try the app too. Later, the analytics showed several active sessions from different phones in Germany: her family, playing together.

**What changed because of it.** Her note about the first-time experience is the kind of feedback that led to CalcQuest's one-round tutorials, which every game now has.

**Why it matters.** One honest reply led to a family playing together, useful feedback and new players by word of mouth. Moments like this are why CalcQuest is built in public.
