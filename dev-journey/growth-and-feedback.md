# CalcQuest growth and player feedback

CalcQuest grew to 25K+ players in 150+ countries, with a 4.9-star rating, without any paid advertising. Growth came from word of mouth, building in public on X, and a steady loop of listening to players and shipping what they asked for. This page describes how feedback reaches the developer and the changes it has driven.

## Growth in numbers

| | |
|---|---|
| Players | 25K+ |
| Countries | 150+ |
| Rating | 4.9 stars |
| Paid advertising | None |
| First release | September 2024 |

## How CalcQuest grows

- **Word of mouth.** Players show the game to friends and family; whole households have ended up playing together. See [Real-world moments](real-world-moments.md).
- **Building in public.** Arin shares progress, numbers and lessons on X as [@ArinBuilds](https://x.com/ArinBuilds).
- **Built-in sharing.** Invite links reward both players, duels can be shared as links, and the daily challenge and profile cards are made to be shared. See [Invite friends](../campaigns/invite-friends.md).
- **Free web puzzles.** Nine puzzles play in a browser at calcquest.app, so a link can turn into a player with no install.
- **Daily habit.** The daily challenge, streaks and [streak rewards](../campaigns/streak-rewards.md) bring players back.

## How feedback reaches the developer

| Channel | Used for |
|---|---|
| Store reviews | Ratings, bug reports, feature ideas; every review is read |
| X ([@ArinBuilds](https://x.com/ArinBuilds)) | Conversations, feedback, development updates |
| Support form and email | Bug reports and account help, through calcquest.app/support and arin@calcquest.app |
| In-app analytics | Which games people play, where they get stuck, where they leave |

## What players asked for, and what shipped

| Players said | What changed |
|---|---|
| "I finished all the levels" | Generated puzzles and endless modes, then 20+ puzzle games |
| "The goal and moves aren't self-explaining the first time" | One-round tutorials for every game |
| "I want to play with my family" | Friend duels, friends and a shared daily challenge |
| "Pick up where I left off" | The Journey saves your place inside a level |
| "More visual feedback when I get it right" | Full-screen celebrations for wins, level-ups and streaks |
| "Sign-up didn't work for me" | Guest-first play: no account needed to start |
| "Multiplayer?" | Asynchronous friend duels in version 2.2 |

## Lessons from the community

- Answering quickly builds trust, even before a fix ships.
- A solo developer cannot build everything; player requests are weighed by how many players they help.
- Players remember the people behind a game. Being a real, reachable person is an advantage.
