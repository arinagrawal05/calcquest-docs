# CalcQuest Premium

CalcQuest Premium is the optional paid plan in CalcQuest. It removes forced ads, unlocks every game and every Journey chapter, gives unlimited duels and a one-time bonus of 500 gems, and adds early access to new features and a Premium badge. It is available as a monthly or yearly subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. CalcQuest stays free to play without it.

## What Premium includes

| Benefit | What it means |
|---|---|
| No forced ads | No ads you did not choose. You can still watch a rewarded ad if you want extra gems. |
| All games unlocked | Every one of the 20+ puzzle games, at every difficulty. |
| Every Journey chapter | The first two chapters of the [Journey](gameplay/journey.md) are free for everyone; Premium opens every chapter after them. |
| Unlimited duels | Challenge as many friends as you like. See [Friend duels](gameplay/duels.md). |
| 500 gems | A one-time bonus when Premium starts, for hints, revives and skips. |
| Early access | New games and features reach Premium members first. |
| Premium badge | A badge on your profile and member card. |

## Plans

| Plan | How it works |
|---|---|
| Monthly | Renews every month until you cancel. |
| Yearly | Renews every 12 months until you cancel; costs less per month than monthly. |
| Lifetime | One payment, no subscription and no renewals. Yours to keep. |

Prices are set in the Google Play Store and the App Store and vary by country; the app shows the price in your currency before you pay. Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time in the store's subscription settings. "Restore purchases" on the Premium screen brings Premium back on a new phone.

## How to get Premium

1. Sign in. Premium is linked to your CalcQuest account, so it is not available to guests.
2. Open Premium from the Journey (a Premium chapter), your Profile, or Settings.
3. Choose a plan and confirm in the store.

## Premium through streak rewards

Players who keep a daily streak can earn Premium for free: 1, 3 or 6 months of CalcQuest Premium are among the rewards at the 14, 30 and 90-day milestones. See [Streak rewards](campaigns/streak-rewards.md).

## What stays free

Premium is a way to support the game and get more of it; it is not required to enjoy it.

- Most puzzle games are free to play, and the first two Journey chapters are free for everyone.
- The daily challenge, streaks, friends and leaderboards are free.
- Ads never interrupt a puzzle, with or without Premium.

See [Monetization ethics](design-philosophy/monetization-ethics.md) for the principles behind this.

## Common questions

**Do hints cost gems with Premium?** Yes. Premium gives 500 gems once, and hints still use gems.

**What happens when a subscription ends?** Premium features stop at the end of the paid period. Your progress, XP and gems stay.

**What if I got Premium from a streak reward?** It ends after its period. The app tells you when it has ended, and you can then choose a plan if you want to keep it.
