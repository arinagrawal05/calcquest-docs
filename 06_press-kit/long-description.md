# Long Description

**Reimagining the calculator.** 
CalcQuest isn't just a game; it's a creative playground where arithmetic becomes the engine for adventure. With 350+ handcrafted levels and infinite procedural challenges, it’s the ultimate destination for puzzle lovers of all ages. 

CalcQuest is an indie mobile puzzle game that reimagines the calculator as a portal to creative problem-solving. Players are presented with a starting number, a target number, and a set of operations. The challenge: reach the target using the available operations within a limited number of moves.

Developed by solo indie developer Arin Agrawal, CalcQuest is built with Flutter for cross-platform availability on Android and iOS.

## Gameplay

The core mechanic is deceptively simple. Each level presents a calculator interface where players tap operations to transform the current number. The twist: solutions often require lateral thinking, not just arithmetic skill. Players must plan sequences of operations, sometimes using non-standard moves like reversing digits or appending numbers.

## Content

CalcQuest offers substantial depth:

- **350+ Handcrafted Levels**: Curated puzzles across multiple worlds, each introducing new mechanics and increasing complexity.
- **10+ Endless Modes**: Procedurally generated challenges for players seeking infinite replayability and high-score competition.
- **Level Editor**: Create custom puzzles and share them with friends via QR codes or share codes.

## Audience

CalcQuest is designed for a broad audience:

- **Casual Puzzle Players**: Engaging brain-teasers in short, satisfying sessions.
- **Families and Educators**: A gamified approach to arithmetic practice.
- **Competitive Players**: Endless modes and leaderboards for those seeking challenge.

## Monetization

CalcQuest is free to play. Advertisements appear at natural break points and can be removed with a one-time in-app purchase. The game contains no pay-to-win mechanics, loot boxes, or subscription requirements.

## Developer

CalcQuest is developed by Arin Agrawal (@ArinBuilds on Twitter/X), an indie developer based in Madhya Pradesh, India. The project reflects a personal mission to make math engaging through thoughtful game design.
