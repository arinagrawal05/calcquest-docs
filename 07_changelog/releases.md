# Release History

**The evolution of a math universe.**
From the first Android beta to the recent iOS launch and the introduction of the level editor, here is the chronological story of every CalcQuest update, feature, and fix.
 It is not a detailed patch-note log but rather a summary of significant updates.

---

## Release History

### Initial Launch

- Launched on Google Play as a free-to-play math puzzle game.
- Included initial set of handcrafted levels.
- Basic campaign progression system.

### Level Editor Update

- Introduced custom level creation.
- Added QR code and share code export for level sharing.
- Enabled community-driven content.

### Endless Modes Expansion

- Added multiple endless/survival mode variants.
- Introduced procedural puzzle generation.
- Implemented high-score tracking for endless sessions.

### iOS Launch

- Expanded platform availability to Apple App Store.
- Integrated Sign in with Apple for iOS authentication.
- Implemented App Tracking Transparency (ATT) compliance.

### Progression and Leaderboard Updates

- Enhanced XP system and progression tracking.
- Integrated Google Play Games Services leaderboards.
- Added cross-device cloud sync for signed-in users.
