# How to play 1024 Twisted

1024 Twisted is a sliding-tile puzzle where you swipe to merge matching numbered tiles and build up to the 1024 tile, with combos for chained merges and a time penalty for swipes that merge nothing. 1024 Twisted is one of the 20+ puzzle games in CalcQuest, the free brain puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web.

## Rules of 1024 Twisted

1. Swipe in any direction to slide every tile. Two identical tiles that meet merge into one.
2. A new tile appears after every swipe.
3. Merges build a combo multiplier for your score.
4. A swipe with no merge breaks the combo and costs one second.
5. The goal is the 1024 tile.

## Tips for 1024 Twisted

- Keep your largest tile in one corner and build outward from it.
- Swipe mostly in two directions, such as right and down, to keep large tiles in place.

## Playing 1024 Twisted in CalcQuest

Open 1024 Twisted from the Games tab, under Puzzle. The first time, a short tutorial walks you through one round. Choose Timed to chase a high score on the leaderboard, or Endless to play with no clock. CalcQuest's puzzle games also appear as stages in the [Journey](../gameplay/journey.md), in the [daily challenge](../gameplay/daily-challenge.md) and in [duels](../gameplay/duels.md).
