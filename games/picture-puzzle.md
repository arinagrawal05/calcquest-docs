# How to play Picture Puzzle

Picture Puzzle is a math puzzle where shapes stand for hidden numbers: you solve a set of picture equations to find each shape's value, then use them to answer the final equation. Picture Puzzle is one of the 20+ puzzle games in CalcQuest, the free brain puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web.

## Rules of Picture Puzzle

1. Each shape stands for a number, and the same shape always has the same value.
2. Work through the given equations to find each shape's value.
3. Use those values to solve the final equation.

## Tips for Picture Puzzle

- Start with an equation that has only one unknown shape.
- Each value you find unlocks the next equation. Substitute it straight away.

## Playing Picture Puzzle in CalcQuest

Open Picture Puzzle from the Games tab, under Puzzle. The first time, a short tutorial walks you through one round. Choose Timed to chase a high score on the leaderboard, or Endless to play with no clock. CalcQuest's puzzle games also appear as stages in the [Journey](../gameplay/journey.md), in the [daily challenge](../gameplay/daily-challenge.md) and in [duels](../gameplay/duels.md).
