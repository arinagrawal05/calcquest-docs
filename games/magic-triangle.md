# How to play Magic Triangle

Magic Triangle is a number puzzle where you place numbers in the circles around a triangle so that every side, corners included, adds up to the same target. Magic Triangle is one of the 20+ puzzle games in CalcQuest, the free brain puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web.

## Rules of Magic Triangle

1. Place numbers in the circles around the triangle.
2. Every side, including the corner circles at each end, must add up to the target.
3. Each number from the panel can be used only once.
4. Tap a circle, then tap a number to place it.

## Tips for Magic Triangle

- Corner circles count for two sides each. Fix the corners first and the middles follow.
- Add up all the numbers in the panel and compare with three times the target: the difference tells you what the corners must add to.

## Playing Magic Triangle in CalcQuest

Open Magic Triangle from the Games tab, under Memory. The first time, a short tutorial walks you through one round. Choose Timed to chase a high score on the leaderboard, or Endless to play with no clock. CalcQuest's puzzle games also appear as stages in the [Journey](../gameplay/journey.md), in the [daily challenge](../gameplay/daily-challenge.md) and in [duels](../gameplay/duels.md).
