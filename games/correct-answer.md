# How to play Correct Answer

Correct Answer is a quick math game where an equation has a missing number and you choose the option that completes it correctly. Correct Answer is one of the 20+ puzzle games in CalcQuest, the free brain puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web.

| | |
|---|---|
| Category | Math |
| Also known as | A missing-number equation game |
| Builds | Arithmetic and inverse operations |
| Difficulties | Easy, Medium, Hard |
| Modes | Timed and Endless |
| Play on the web | App only |

## Rules of Correct Answer

1. An incomplete equation appears, such as ? + 4 = 9.
2. Pick the option that correctly fills the gap.

## Tips for Correct Answer

- Rule out options that are clearly too large or too small first.
- Work backwards: for ? × 3 = 12, ask what times 3 makes 12.

## Playing Correct Answer in CalcQuest

Open Correct Answer from the Games tab, under Math. The first time, a short tutorial walks you through one round. Choose Timed to chase a high score on the leaderboard, or Endless to play with no clock. CalcQuest's puzzle games also appear as stages in the [Journey](../gameplay/journey.md), in the [daily challenge](../gameplay/daily-challenge.md) and in [duels](../gameplay/duels.md).
