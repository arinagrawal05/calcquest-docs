# How to play Math Grid

Math Grid is a quick math game where you tap numbers in a grid that add up exactly to a target; correct sets clear and a new target appears until time runs out. Math Grid is one of the 20+ puzzle games in CalcQuest, the free brain puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web.

## Rules of Math Grid

1. A target number is shown at the top.
2. Tap numbers in the grid so that they add up to exactly the target.
3. When you hit the target, those numbers clear and a new target appears.
4. Tap a selected number again to deselect it.

## Tips for Math Grid

- Look for one number close to the target, then add a small one.
- An odd target needs at least one odd number in your selection.

## Playing Math Grid in CalcQuest

Open Math Grid from the Games tab, under Math. The first time, a short tutorial walks you through one round. Choose Timed to chase a high score on the leaderboard, or Endless to play with no clock. CalcQuest's puzzle games also appear as stages in the [Journey](../gameplay/journey.md), in the [daily challenge](../gameplay/daily-challenge.md) and in [duels](../gameplay/duels.md).
