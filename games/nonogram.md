# How to play Nonogram

Nonogram is a picture logic puzzle where you fill cells in a grid using the number clues beside each row and above each column, which tell you the lengths of the filled runs in that line. Nonogram is one of the 20+ puzzle games in CalcQuest, the free brain puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web.

## Rules of Nonogram

1. The clue beside a row or above a column lists the runs of filled cells in that line, in order.
2. "3 1" means a run of 3 filled cells, at least one empty cell, then a run of 1.
3. Runs must appear in the listed order and must not touch.
4. Tap to fill a cell; tap again or long-press to mark it empty.

## Tips for Nonogram

- If a single clue is more than half the line, the middle cells are filled whatever its position.
- Lines with only one possible arrangement are the best place to start.
- Marking empty cells matters as much as filling: it narrows every crossing line.
