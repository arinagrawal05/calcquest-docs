# How to play Math Pairs

Math Pairs is a memory and math game where face-down cards hide equations and answers; you flip two at a time to match each equation with its correct answer. Math Pairs is one of the 20+ puzzle games in CalcQuest, the free brain puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web.

## Rules of Math Pairs

1. Cards lie face down. Each hides an equation or a number that answers one of the equations.
2. Flip two cards. An equation and its answer make a match and are removed.
3. Cards that do not match flip back over.

## Tips for Math Pairs

- Solve each equation the moment you see it, so you know which answer card to look for.
- Flip in a steady pattern across the board to see as many cards as possible.

## Playing Math Pairs in CalcQuest

Open Math Pairs from the Games tab, under Math. The first time, a short tutorial walks you through one round. Choose Timed to chase a high score on the leaderboard, or Endless to play with no clock. CalcQuest's puzzle games also appear as stages in the [Journey](../gameplay/journey.md), in the [daily challenge](../gameplay/daily-challenge.md) and in [duels](../gameplay/duels.md).
