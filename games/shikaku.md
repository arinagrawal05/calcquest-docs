# How to play Shikaku

Shikaku is a grid puzzle where you divide the board into rectangles, each containing exactly one number equal to its area, so that every cell is covered and no rectangles overlap. Shikaku is one of the 20+ puzzle games in CalcQuest, the free brain puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web.

## Rules of Shikaku

1. Every number needs one rectangle drawn around it whose area (width × height) equals the number.
2. Each rectangle contains exactly one number.
3. Every cell is covered, and no two rectangles overlap.
4. Tap a numbered cell, then drag to draw its rectangle.

## Tips for Shikaku

- Start with the biggest numbers: large areas have the fewest possible shapes.
- A prime number such as 5 or 7 can only be a one-cell-wide strip.
- A 1 is its own 1×1 rectangle. Place it and move on.
