# How to play Binairo

Binairo is a logic puzzle where you fill a grid with 0s and 1s so that every row and column has equal numbers of each, no three identical digits sit in a row, and no two rows or columns are the same. Binairo is one of the 20+ puzzle games in CalcQuest, the free brain puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web.

## Rules of Binairo

1. Fill every empty cell with a 0 or a 1.
2. Every row and every column has the same number of 0s and 1s.
3. No three identical digits may sit next to each other in a row or column.
4. No two rows are identical, and no two columns are identical.
5. Pre-filled cells are clues and cannot be changed.

## Tips for Binairo

- If a row already has its full count of one digit, every remaining cell is the other digit.
- Next to a pair of identical digits, both neighbours must be the opposite digit.
- If a row is nearly the same as a finished row, at least one cell must differ, and the rules will show which.
