# How to play Minesweeper

Minesweeper is a logic game where you uncover every safe cell on a board without touching a hidden mine, using numbers that tell you how many mines touch each cell. Minesweeper is one of the 20+ puzzle games in CalcQuest, the free brain puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web.

## Rules of Minesweeper

1. Mines are hidden under some cells. Tap a cell to reveal it.
2. A number shows how many mines are in the eight cells around it. A 0 reveals its neighbours automatically.
3. Long-press a cell to flag it as a mine.
4. You win by revealing every safe cell.

## Tips for Minesweeper

- If a number equals the count of its hidden neighbours, they are all mines. Flag them.
- Once all mines around a number are flagged, every other neighbour is safe to open.
- Start near the centre: a first tap there usually opens a large safe area.
