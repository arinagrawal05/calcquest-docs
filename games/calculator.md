# How to play Calculator

Calculator is a mental math game where an equation appears on screen and you work out the answer in your head, then type it on the keypad. Calculator is one of the 20+ puzzle games in CalcQuest, the free brain puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web.

## Rules of Calculator

1. An equation appears. Calculate the result mentally.
2. Type the answer on the keypad and submit.
3. Wrong answers cost points, so a quick check is worth it.

## Tips for Calculator

- Break multiplications into easy parts: 12 × 8 = 10 × 8 + 2 × 8 = 96.
- With brackets, always work out the inside first.

## Playing Calculator in CalcQuest

Open Calculator from the Games tab, under Math. The first time, a short tutorial walks you through one round. Choose Timed to chase a high score on the leaderboard, or Endless to play with no clock. CalcQuest's puzzle games also appear as stages in the [Journey](../gameplay/journey.md), in the [daily challenge](../gameplay/daily-challenge.md) and in [duels](../gameplay/duels.md).
