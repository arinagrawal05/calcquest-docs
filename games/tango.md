# How to play Tango

Tango is a logic puzzle where you fill a grid with suns and moons so that every row and column has equal numbers of each, never three alike in a row, and every = and × sign between cells is respected. ## Rules of Tango

1. Fill every empty cell with a sun or a moon. Tap once for one symbol, again to switch.
2. Every row and every column has the same number of suns and moons. A 6-cell row needs 3 of each.
3. No more than two identical symbols may sit next to each other in a row or column.
4. An = sign between two cells means they show the same symbol; an × sign means they differ.

## Tips for Tango

- A chain of = signs means all those cells match. Solve one and you have solved them all.
- Two identical symbols side by side? The cells at both ends of the pair must be the other symbol.
- Count as you go: once a row has 3 suns, every other cell in it is a moon.
