# How to play Rootdown

Rootdown is a quick math game where a number appears and you enter its square root; every answer is a whole number. Rootdown is one of the 20+ puzzle games in CalcQuest, the free brain puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web.

| | |
|---|---|
| Category | Memory |

| Builds | Number recall and mental math |
| Difficulties | Easy, Medium, Hard |
| Modes | Timed and Endless |
| Play on the web | App only |

## Rules of Rootdown

1. A number appears. Enter its square root: the number that, multiplied by itself, gives it.
2. All answers are whole numbers.

## Tips for Rootdown

- Learn the first twelve perfect squares: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, 36, 49, 64, 81, 100, 121, 144.
- Narrow it down: a number between 64 and 81 has a root between 8 and 9.

## Playing Rootdown in CalcQuest

Open Rootdown from the Games tab, under Memory. The first time, a short tutorial walks you through one round. Choose Timed to chase a high score on the leaderboard, or Endless to play with no clock. CalcQuest's puzzle games also appear as stages in the [Journey](../gameplay/journey.md), in the [daily challenge](../gameplay/daily-challenge.md) and in [duels](../gameplay/duels.md).
