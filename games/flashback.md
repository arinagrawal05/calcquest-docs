# How to play Flashback

Flashback is a memory game where a pattern of tiles lights up for a few seconds and then disappears; you tap the same tiles from memory to recreate it. Flashback is one of the 20+ puzzle games in CalcQuest, the free brain puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web.

|---|---|
| Category | Memory |

## Rules of Flashback

1. A pattern of tiles lights up for a few seconds, then disappears.
2. Tap the tiles that were lit to recreate the pattern.
3. Tapping a tile that was not lit counts as a mistake. There is no rush when recalling.

## Tips for Flashback

- Remember shapes, not single cells: "an L in the top right" is easier to hold.
- Split the pattern into two or three chunks and recall them one at a time.

## Playing Flashback in CalcQuest

Open Flashback from the Games tab, under Memory. The first time, a short tutorial walks you through one round. Choose Timed to chase a high score on the leaderboard, or Endless to play with no clock. CalcQuest's puzzle games also appear as stages in the [Journey](../gameplay/journey.md), in the [daily challenge](../gameplay/daily-challenge.md) and in [duels](../gameplay/duels.md).
