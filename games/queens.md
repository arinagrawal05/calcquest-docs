# How to play Queens

Queens is a logic puzzle where you place exactly one queen in every coloured region of the board, so that no two queens share a row, a column, or touch, even diagonally. Queens is one of the 20+ puzzle games in CalcQuest, the free brain puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web.

## Rules of Queens

1. The board is divided into coloured regions. Place exactly one queen in each region.
2. No two queens may share a row or a column.
3. No two queens may touch, not even corner to corner.
4. Tap a cell to place a queen and tap again to remove it. Every board has exactly one solution.

## Tips for Queens

- Start with the smallest region. The fewer cells it has, the fewer places its queen can go.
- When you place a queen, its whole row, its column and every cell around it are ruled out for other queens.
- If only one cell in a region is free of conflicts, the queen must go there.
