# How to play Number Alchemy

Number Alchemy is a number puzzle where you combine a set of given numbers with +, −, × and ÷ to reach a target exactly, using each number at most once. Number Alchemy is one of the 20+ puzzle games in CalcQuest, the free brain puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web.

## Rules of Number Alchemy

1. You get a set of numbers and a target.
2. Combine numbers with +, −, × or ÷ to reach the target exactly.
3. Each number can be used at most once, and you do not have to use them all.
4. Normal order of operations applies: × and ÷ before + and −.

## Tips for Number Alchemy

- For a large target, start with a multiplication to get into range quickly.
- Work backwards: which single operation on the target brings it closer to the numbers you have?

## Playing Number Alchemy in CalcQuest

Open Number Alchemy from the Games tab, under Logic. The first time, a short tutorial walks you through one round. Choose Timed to chase a high score on the leaderboard, or Endless to play with no clock. CalcQuest's puzzle games also appear as stages in the [Journey](../gameplay/journey.md), in the [daily challenge](../gameplay/daily-challenge.md) and in [duels](../gameplay/duels.md).
