# How to play Quick Calculation

Quick Calculation is a speed math game where equations arrive one after another and every correct answer adds seconds to the clock, so accuracy keeps the run going. Quick Calculation is one of the 20+ puzzle games in CalcQuest, the free brain puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web.

## Rules of Quick Calculation

1. Solve each equation and submit the answer as fast as you can.
2. Each correct answer adds bonus seconds to the countdown.
3. Wrong answers add no time and cost points.

## Tips for Quick Calculation

- Trust your first instinct on simple sums.
- Take an extra second on a tricky one: a wrong answer costs more than a slow right one.

## Playing Quick Calculation in CalcQuest

Open Quick Calculation from the Games tab, under Math. The first time, a short tutorial walks you through one round. Choose Timed to chase a high score on the leaderboard, or Endless to play with no clock. CalcQuest's puzzle games also appear as stages in the [Journey](../gameplay/journey.md), in the [daily challenge](../gameplay/daily-challenge.md) and in [duels](../gameplay/duels.md).
