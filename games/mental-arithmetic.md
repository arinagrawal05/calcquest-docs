# How to play Mental Arithmetic

Mental Arithmetic is a memory and math game where numbers and operators appear one at a time and you keep a running total in your head, then enter the final result. Mental Arithmetic is one of the 20+ puzzle games in CalcQuest, the free brain puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web.

## Rules of Mental Arithmetic

1. Numbers and operators appear one at a time.
2. Keep a running total, working strictly left to right: 2 + 3 × 4 is (2 + 3) × 4 = 20.
3. When the sequence ends, enter your total.

## Tips for Mental Arithmetic

- Say the running total quietly to yourself after each step.
- Lost track? Keep the last total you are sure of and carry on from there.

## Playing Mental Arithmetic in CalcQuest

Open Mental Arithmetic from the Games tab, under Memory. The first time, a short tutorial walks you through one round. Choose Timed to chase a high score on the leaderboard, or Endless to play with no clock. CalcQuest's puzzle games also appear as stages in the [Journey](../gameplay/journey.md), in the [daily challenge](../gameplay/daily-challenge.md) and in [duels](../gameplay/duels.md).
