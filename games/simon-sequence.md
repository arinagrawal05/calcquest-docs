# How to play Simon Sequence

Simon Sequence is a memory game where tiles light up one after another and you repeat the sequence in the same order; each round adds one more tile. Simon Sequence is one of the 20+ puzzle games in CalcQuest, the free brain puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web.

## Rules of Simon Sequence

1. Watch the tiles light up one by one.
2. When the sequence ends, tap the tiles in the same order.
3. Each round adds one new tile to the end of the sequence.
4. Tapping before the sequence finishes counts as a mistake.

## Tips for Simon Sequence

- Group the tiles into chunks of two or three.
- Focus on the newest tile: the earlier ones should already be familiar.

Open Simon Sequence from the Games tab, under Memory. The first time, a short tutorial walks you through one round. Choose Timed to chase a high score on the leaderboard, or Endless to play with no clock.
