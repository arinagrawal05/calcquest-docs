# CalcQuest glossary

Definitions of the terms used in CalcQuest and in these docs, from Journey and duels to gems, streaks and Premium. Terms are in alphabetical order.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Achievement** | A Google Play Games badge on Android, unlocked by Journey milestones such as clearing 10, 25 or 50 levels. |
| **Avatar** | Your profile picture in CalcQuest. Some avatars are unlocked with gems. |
| **Brain puzzle game** | The category CalcQuest belongs to: a game of short logic, number and memory puzzles. |
| **CalcQuest** | The free brain puzzle game for Android, iOS and the web. Always written as one word with a capital C and Q. |
| **CalcQuest Premium** | The optional paid plan: no forced ads, every game and Journey chapter, unlimited duels, 500 gems, early access and a badge. Monthly, yearly or lifetime. |
| **Calculator Classic** | The original CalcQuest game: reach a target number with a limited number of calculator presses, across 350+ handcrafted levels. |
| **Category** | One of the four groups of games: Logic, Puzzle, Math and Memory. |
| **Chapter** | A section of the Journey with 10 levels, its own name and colour, and a trophy at the end. |
| **Chest** | A reward point inside a Journey chapter that gives bonus gems. |
| **Daily challenge** | Four puzzle games, the same for every player each day, played against a stopwatch, with a daily leaderboard. |
| **Difficulty** | Easy, Medium or Hard. Also rises every few rounds inside a session. |
| **Duel** | A race between friends on the same puzzle board. Each plays in their own time; the fastest time within 48 hours wins. |
| **Endless** | A mode with no clock and no limit, for relaxed play. |
| **Gems** | CalcQuest's in-game currency, earned by playing and spent on hints, revives, stage skips and avatars. |
| **Guest** | A player who has not signed in. Guests can play every game, the Journey and the daily challenge. |
| **Handcrafted level** | A level designed and solved by hand, as in the 350+ levels of Calculator Classic. |
| **Hint** | A nudge toward the next step of a puzzle, costing 5 gems. |
| **Invite link** | A personal link that gives a new player 20 gems and the inviter 10. |
| **Journey** | CalcQuest's main mode: a map of chapters whose levels are short runs of mixed puzzle stages. |
| **Leaderboard** | A ranking by best Timed score in a game, or by total time on the daily challenge. |
| **Player level** | Your level, raised by XP. Each level needs level × 50 + 100 XP. |
| **Revive** | Continuing a run after a mistake or time-out, paid with gems or an optional rewarded ad. |
| **Rewarded ad** | A short video ad you choose to watch in exchange for gems or a revive. |
| **Stage** | One puzzle board inside a Journey level. |
| **Streak** | The number of days in a row on which you played. Needs a signed-in account. |
| **Streak reward** | A real reward claimed at a 14, 30, 90, 180 or 365-day streak, such as months of Premium. |
| **Timed** | A mode where you score as many points as you can before a countdown ends. |
| **Title** | A profile rank earned with total XP, up to Pro and Master. Not the same as CalcQuest Premium. |
| **Trophy** | The reward for finishing a Journey chapter. |
| **Tutorial** | A one-round introduction to a game's rules, shown the first time you play it. |
| **XP** | Experience points, earned from correct answers, Journey levels and duel wins. XP raises your player level. |
