## How to Add Citations

When adding a new citation, include:

1. **Source Name**: Publication, platform, or author
2. **Date**: When the content was published
3. **Title**: Title of the article or post
4. **URL**: Link to the source
5. **Summary**: One-sentence description of the content

Example format:

```
### [Source Name] - [Date]
**Title**: [Article Title]
**URL**: [Link]
**Summary**: [Brief description]
```
