# Who CalcQuest is for

CalcQuest is for anyone who wants a short, satisfying brain workout instead of another round of scrolling. Most players fall into two groups: adults who play a few logic puzzles a day to stay sharp, and students aged 11 and up who enjoy math and logic. Both play the same app; this page explains what each group gets from it.

## Adults who want a daily brain workout

You have five minutes on a commute, a coffee break or before bed, and you would rather spend them on something that leaves you sharper than a feed does.

- **Short sessions.** A Journey level or a daily challenge takes a few minutes, and the game is designed to let you stop.
- **Puzzles you may know.** [Queens](games/queens.md), [Tango](games/tango.md) and [Mini Sudoku](games/mini-sudoku.md), the logic puzzles made popular by LinkedIn and newspapers, with unlimited boards instead of one a day.
- **A habit with a reason to return.** Daily streaks, the [daily challenge](gameplay/daily-challenge.md), and real rewards in the [streak reward program](campaigns/streak-rewards.md).
- **Something to share.** [Duels](gameplay/duels.md) with friends and colleagues, and a daily leaderboard among people you know.

Good starting games: Queens, Tango, Mini Sudoku, Shikaku, Nonogram, Minesweeper.

## Students who like math and logic

CalcQuest suits students aged 11 and up who like puzzles, or who want arithmetic to feel like a game rather than a worksheet.

- **Math puzzles, not drills.** [KenKen](games/kenken.md), [Number Pyramid](games/number-pyramid.md), [Math Grid](games/math-grid.md) and [Calculator Classic](games/calculator-classic.md) ask you to think, not to recite tables.
- **Mental speed when you want it.** [Quick Calculation](games/quick-calculation.md) and [Mental Arithmetic](games/mental-arithmetic.md) train fast, accurate calculation.
- **Memory and focus.** [Flashback](games/flashback.md) and [Simon Sequence](games/simon-sequence.md) build working memory.
- **Safe to start.** Students can play as a guest with no account and no personal details. Ads never interrupt a puzzle, and there are no loot boxes.

Good starting games: Calculator Classic, KenKen, Number Pyramid, Math Grid, Magic Triangle.

## Families

Parents and children can play the same daily challenge and compare times, or send each other duels. Every game has Easy, Medium and Hard, so a child and an adult can play side by side at their own level. Purchases always go through the store's own confirmation.

## Teachers and educators

CalcQuest works as a warm-up or a reward: the math and logic games exercise reasoning, arithmetic and memory, and nine puzzles run free in a browser at [calcquest.app](https://calcquest.app) with no install. For school programmes, see [calcquest.app/education](https://calcquest.app/education).

## Puzzle fans

If you already play Sudoku, KenKen, Nonograms or Minesweeper, CalcQuest gives you 20+ puzzle types in one app, with Hard boards, timed high scores and leaderboards. If you liked *Calculator: The Game*, [Calculator Classic](games/calculator-classic.md) offers 350+ handcrafted "reach the target" levels.

## Who it is not for

- People looking for a calculator app. CalcQuest is a game.
- People who want a long, story-driven game. CalcQuest is built for short sessions.
- Players who want live, real-time multiplayer. Duels are asynchronous: each player plays in their own time.
