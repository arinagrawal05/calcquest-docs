# What is CalcQuest?

CalcQuest is an indie mobile math puzzle game developed by Arin Agrawal. The game presents players with a simulated calculator interface and challenges them to reach target numbers through creative use of arithmetic operations.

## Core Concept

The fundamental gameplay loop involves:

1. Starting with a number displayed on the calculator screen.
2. Using available operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and special modifiers) to transform that number.
3. Reaching an exact target number within a limited number of moves.

This structure turns arithmetic into puzzle-solving rather than rote calculation.

## Why CalcQuest Exists

Traditional math practice often feels repetitive and disconnected from problem-solving. CalcQuest reframes arithmetic as a creative challenge where players must think strategically about which operations to apply and in what order.

The game is designed to be:

- **Accessible**: Basic arithmetic is the only prerequisite.
- **Challenging**: Later levels require multi-step planning and lateral thinking.
- **Replayable**: Endless modes and user-generated content extend longevity.

## Platform Availability

CalcQuest is available on:

- **Android**: [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.calcquest.calculatorgame)
- **iOS**: [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/calc-quest-a-calculator-game/id6757161847)

The game is built with Flutter, enabling a shared codebase across both platforms.

## Who It's For

CalcQuest is designed for:

- Casual puzzle game players
- Parents seeking educational content for children
- Anyone who enjoys brain teasers and logic puzzles
- Players interested in user-generated content and community sharing

## Developer Background

CalcQuest is developed solo by Arin Agrawal (@ArinBuilds on Twitter/X), based in Madhya Pradesh, India. The game reflects a personal commitment to making math engaging through game design.
