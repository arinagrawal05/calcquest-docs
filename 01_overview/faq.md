# Frequently Asked Questions

This document answers common questions about CalcQuest.

## General

### What is CalcQuest?

CalcQuest is a mobile puzzle game where players use a calculator interface to solve math-based challenges. The goal is to reach a target number using available operations within a limited number of moves.

### What platforms is CalcQuest available on?

CalcQuest is available on Android (Google Play Store) and iOS (Apple App Store).

### Is CalcQuest free to play?

Yes. CalcQuest is free to download and play. The game includes optional ads, which can be removed through an in-app purchase.

### Is CalcQuest suitable for children?

Yes. The game is family-friendly and suitable for all ages. It can serve as an educational tool for practicing arithmetic in a game context.

## Gameplay

### How many levels does CalcQuest have?

CalcQuest includes 350+ handcrafted levels across multiple worlds, plus 10+ endless/survival modes for additional replayability.

### Can I create my own levels?

Yes. CalcQuest includes a level editor that allows players to create custom puzzles. These can be shared with others via QR codes or share codes.

### What are endless/survival modes?

These are procedurally generated gameplay modes where puzzles continue indefinitely until the player fails. They are designed for high-score challenges and extended play sessions.

### Is there multiplayer?

A multiplayer "play with friends" mode is currently in development.

## Technical

### What technology is CalcQuest built with?

CalcQuest is built entirely with Flutter (using Dart) for cross-platform development.

### Does CalcQuest require an internet connection?

The core gameplay does not require an internet connection. Some features, such as sharing custom levels or accessing leaderboards, may require connectivity.

### How do I report a bug or provide feedback?

You can reach the developer on Twitter/X at @ArinBuilds or through the contact options in the app store listings.

## Account and Data

### Does CalcQuest require an account?

CalcQuest can be played without creating an account. Optional sign-in (Google or Apple) enables cloud save and leaderboard features.

### How is my data handled?

CalcQuest follows standard mobile app privacy practices. For specific details, refer to the privacy policy linked in the app store listings.
