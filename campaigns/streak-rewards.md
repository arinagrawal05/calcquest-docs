# CalcQuest streak reward program

The CalcQuest streak reward program gives real rewards to players who keep a daily streak. When your streak reaches 14, 30, 90, 180 or 365 days, you claim one reward for that milestone: CalcQuest Premium time for players everywhere, or a one-month partner subscription such as JioHotstar, Spotify, YouTube Premium or Netflix for players in India. At 365 days the reward is CalcQuest Premium for life. Nothing is ever paid by the player.

## The reward ladder

| Streak | Milestone | Choose one reward |
|---|---|---|
| 14 days | 2 weeks strong | CalcQuest Premium, 1 month |
| 30 days | A whole month | CalcQuest Premium, 3 months, or JioHotstar Mobile, 1 month (India) |
| 90 days | 90 days. Unstoppable. | CalcQuest Premium, 6 months, or Spotify Premium, 1 month (India), or YouTube Premium, 1 month (India) |
| 180 days | Half a year | CalcQuest Premium, 6 months, or Netflix (mobile plan), 1 month (India) |
| 365 days | A full year | CalcQuest Premium, Lifetime |

Players outside India see the CalcQuest Premium options. The live ladder and full terms are at [calcquest.app/streak-reward-program-terms](https://calcquest.app/streak-reward-program-terms); if they ever differ from this page, the terms page applies.

## How a streak counts

- A streak is the number of consecutive calendar days on which you completed at least one puzzle.
- CalcQuest's servers decide eligibility from your activity record, not from the number on your phone. Changed device clocks or edited data do not count.
- Your best run counts. If you already have a long streak, you can claim every milestone it has reached.

## How to claim

1. Sign in. Guests have no streak record, so they cannot claim.
2. Open the Streak screen. The Streak Rewards timeline shows every milestone with markers for your current and best streak.
3. Tap a milestone you have reached and choose your reward. The choice is final.
4. **CalcQuest Premium** switches on in your account at once.
5. **A partner subscription** takes you to a short form on calcquest.app to confirm your name, delivery email and LinkedIn profile (a phone number is optional). The claim shows as Preparing in the app; CalcQuest then contacts you, usually within 48 hours, and pays for the subscription on your behalf.

## The rules in brief

- Each milestone pays once per account, for life. A streak that breaks and is rebuilt does not earn the same milestone twice.
- A partner reward is one month of the named plan; it is not an annual or family plan and does not renew.
- Rewards have no cash value and cannot be exchanged, transferred or sold.
- One person, one account. Cheating, multiple accounts, automated play or tampering with streak data cancels rewards.
- CalcQuest is not affiliated with or endorsed by the partner services; their names belong to their owners.
- The milestones, rewards and regions may change. Claims already made are honoured.

## Why CalcQuest runs it

A daily puzzle habit is the point of CalcQuest: a few minutes a day that leave you sharper than scrolling would. The streak reward program thanks the players who build that habit, with rewards they already use. It costs the player nothing: no card, no top-up, no catch.

## Questions

**When is a reward delivered?** Premium is instant. Partner subscriptions are usually arranged within 48 hours of the form.

**What happens when reward Premium ends?** The app tells you, and your progress, XP and gems stay. You can choose a Premium plan if you want to keep it. See [CalcQuest Premium](../premium.md).

**I already have Premium. Can I still claim?** Premium options are hidden while you have Premium, so you will see the partner options where they are available.

**Who do I contact?** Email [arin@calcquest.app](mailto:arin@calcquest.app) and mention your milestone day.
