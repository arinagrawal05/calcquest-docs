# Invite friends to CalcQuest

Every CalcQuest player has a personal invite link and referral code. When a friend joins through it, the friend gets 20 gems and you get 10. The link works whether or not your friend already has the app: without it, they go to the store, and the invite is kept through the install.

## How it works

1. Open **Invite friends** from your profile or the Games tab.
2. Share your link on WhatsApp, Instagram, X or anywhere else, or share your referral code.
3. Your friend opens the link, or types the code on the welcome screen.
4. When they join, they receive 20 gems and you receive 10.

## What your friend sees

- The link opens `calcquest.app/invite/<your code>`, which opens the CalcQuest app if it is installed, or the right store if it is not.
- After installing, the app remembers who invited them, so the code is filled in automatically and the welcome screen shows who referred them.
- They can start playing at once as a guest. See [Play as a guest](../gameplay/guest-play.md).

## After your friend joins

Add each other as friends in CalcQuest, and you can:

- compare times on the [daily challenge](../gameplay/daily-challenge.md),
- send each other [duels](../gameplay/duels.md),
- see each other on friends leaderboards.

## Rules

- Inviting needs a signed-in account.
- The gems are paid once per new player.
- Codes are personal; self-referrals and fake accounts do not earn gems.
