**From a simple observation to 350+ levels.** 
What if the processes of reaching an answer became the game itself? CalcQuest began as a solo experiment by Arin Agrawal to turn the humble calculator into an engine for "aha!" moments. Here is the story of how that journey started.

## Background

CalcQuest is developed by Arin Agrawal, a solo indie developer based in Madhya Pradesh, India. The project began as an exploration of how arithmetic could be transformed from a rote activity into an engaging puzzle experience.

## Motivation

The core idea emerged from a simple observation: calculators are tools for computing answers, but what if the process of reaching an answer became the game itself?

Traditional math education often emphasizes speed and accuracy in isolation. CalcQuest reframes arithmetic as a creative challenge where the journey matters as much as the destination.

## Development Approach

The game is built entirely with Flutter (Dart), chosen for:

- Cross-platform capability (single codebase for Android and iOS).
- Rapid iteration during development.
- Strong community and documentation.

Development follows an iterative pattern: design levels, playtest, refine, and expand. Community feedback directly influences updates and new content.

## Solo Development

CalcQuest is a solo project. This means:

- Design, development, art, and marketing are handled by one person.
- Decisions can be made quickly without coordination overhead.
- Resource constraints require prioritization and scope management.

The solo nature also creates a direct connection between the developer and the community. Feedback is received and acted upon without layers of abstraction.

## Timeline

Specific milestones are not detailed here to maintain timelessness. The game has evolved from an initial concept through multiple content expansions and feature additions, including the level editor, endless modes, and platform expansion to iOS.

## Ongoing Development

CalcQuest continues to receive updates. Planned features include multiplayer functionality ("play with friends" mode), which is currently in development.
