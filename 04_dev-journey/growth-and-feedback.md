**10,000+ installs and counting.**
Building in public means listening as much as coding. From viral moments on social media to critical feedback that reshaped our onboarding, this is the story of how the community helped CalcQuest grow.

## Community Engagement Philosophy

CalcQuest prioritizes direct communication between the developer and players. Feedback is treated as a valuable input that shapes the game's direction.

## Feedback Channels

### App Store Reviews

Reviews on Google Play and the App Store provide insights into user satisfaction and pain points. Both positive feedback and constructive criticism inform development priorities.

### Social Media

The developer maintains a presence on Twitter/X (@ArinBuilds), where players can share feedback, ask questions, and engage with development updates.

### In-App Feedback

The game includes mechanisms for users to report issues or provide suggestions directly.

## How Feedback Shapes Development

### Feature Requests

Community suggestions have influenced feature development, including:

- Expansion of endless mode variants.
- Improvements to the level editor.
- Quality-of-life changes such as UI adjustments.

### Bug Reports

User reports of technical issues are prioritized for investigation and resolution.

### Content Preferences

Player responses to different puzzle types and difficulty levels inform the design of new content.

## Growth Indicators

CalcQuest has achieved:

- 10,000+ installs on Google Play.
- A high average rating (approximately 4.9 stars, though this may vary by region and update cycle).
- Expansion to iOS via the Apple App Store.

These metrics reflect organic growth driven by word-of-mouth and store discoverability rather than large-scale marketing campaigns.

## Lessons from Community Interaction

- Players appreciate responsiveness. Even acknowledging feedback builds trust.
- Feature scope must balance player requests with solo development capacity.
- Positive reviews often highlight the creative puzzle design and family-friendly nature.

## Ongoing Engagement

Community feedback continues to be a core input. New features, including the upcoming multiplayer mode, are developed with player expectations in mind.
