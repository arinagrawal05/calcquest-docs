# Progress and rewards in CalcQuest

CalcQuest tracks your progress with XP and player levels, rewards play with gems, and builds a daily habit with streaks. Players who keep a long streak can also claim real rewards, such as months of CalcQuest Premium, through the streak reward program. All of it is earned by playing; nothing requires spending money.

## XP and player levels

- You earn XP for correct answers in the puzzle games, 50 XP for each cleared Journey level, and 50 XP for each duel you win.
- XP fills your player level. Each level needs a little more XP than the one before: level × 50 + 100.
- Some levels pay bonus gems when you reach them.
- As your total XP grows you earn profile titles, from beginner ranks up to Pro and Master.
- Level-ups and new titles play a full-screen celebration.

## Gems

Gems are CalcQuest's in-game currency.

| Earn gems from | Spend gems on |
|---|---|
| Daily and comeback rewards | Hints (5 gems each) |
| XP level milestones | Revives, to continue a run |
| Clearing Journey levels, chests and trophies | Skipping a Journey stage |
| Inviting friends (10 gems; your friend gets 20) | Avatars |
| Optional rewarded videos | |
| Gem packs and the 500-gem Premium bonus | |

## Daily streaks

- A day counts when you play: finish a puzzle game with a real score, finish a Journey level, or complete the daily challenge.
- The streak screen shows your last seven days as eggs. Miss a day and the streak resets, with a cracking-egg animation.
- Days you play offline still count; they are saved on the phone and synced later.
- Streaks need a signed-in account, so they can be kept safely across devices.

## Streak rewards

At 14, 30, 90, 180 and 365 days of streak you can claim a real reward, once per milestone: CalcQuest Premium time for everyone, or a partner subscription in India. See [Streak rewards](../campaigns/streak-rewards.md).

## Leaderboards

| Leaderboard | Ranked by |
|---|---|
| Per game | Best Timed score |
| Daily challenge | Best total time today |
| Friends | The same boards, filtered to people you know |
| Google Play Games (Android) | Category leaderboards for Math, Logic, Memory and Puzzle |

## Achievements

On Android, Journey milestones (such as clearing 10, 25 and 50 levels) unlock Google Play Games achievements.

## Profile

Your profile shows your avatar, level and title, total XP, streak, duel record and per-game stats. You can share it as a profile card with a link that opens your profile at calcquest.app.
