# Friend duels in CalcQuest

A CalcQuest duel is a race against a friend on the exact same puzzle. You solve a board first, then challenge a friend to that board; they play in their own time, and the fastest time wins. A duel ends after 48 hours, or earlier once both players have played. The winner earns XP, and wins, losses and win streaks show on your profile.

## How to start a duel

1. Open the Duels tab, or a friend's profile.
2. Pick a game and difficulty, and solve the board.
3. Choose the friend to challenge. They get a notification with your time to beat.
4. You can also share a duel link. Anyone who opens it can play the same board; guests are asked to sign in first, then land back on the duel.

## How to answer a duel

- Open the challenge from the notification or the Duels tab. The game and difficulty are locked to the challenger's.
- Tap **Accept challenge** to play, or **Not now** to decline.
- Play the board. Your time is compared with theirs.

## Who wins

| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| Both players finish | The faster time wins |
| Exactly the same time | A shared win for both |
| Only one player finishes within 48 hours | No winner; the duel does not count for either player |
| The friend declines | The duel ends and does not count |

The winner receives 50 XP. Only duels with at least two finishers count toward wins, losses and win streaks.

## Your duel record

Your profile keeps your wins, losses, current win streak and best win streak. The Duels tab shows whose turn it is, the duels waiting for you, and your recent results.

## Fair play

Duel results are decided on CalcQuest's servers, not on the phone. Each duel uses a seeded board, so both players get the identical puzzle.

## Duels and Premium

Duels are free to play. [CalcQuest Premium](../premium.md) gives unlimited duels.

## Requirements

Duels need a signed-in account and an internet connection, because the result is checked on the server and your friend is notified.
