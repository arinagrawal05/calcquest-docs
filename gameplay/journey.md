# The CalcQuest Journey

The Journey is CalcQuest's main mode: a winding map of chapters, each with 10 levels. Every level is a short run of different puzzle games, so a single level might ask for a Queens board, a quick calculation and a memory pattern. Clearing a level moves you along the map and earns 50 XP and a gem, with chests and trophies along the way. The first two chapters are free; later chapters are part of CalcQuest Premium.

## How a Journey level works

1. Tap the next level on the map.
2. The level opens as a vertical run of puzzle stages. Each stage is one board from one of the 20+ games.
3. Solve each stage. There is no clock; take the time you need.
4. Stuck? Use a hint, or skip the stage for gems.
5. When every stage is solved, the level is cleared and the next one opens.

Your place in a level is saved, so you can leave and come back.

## Chapters

The Journey is grouped into chapters of 10 levels, each with its own name and colour: Owl Orchard, Maple Ridge, Cedar Hill, Willow Creek, Birch Meadow and Aspen Grove, and then onward. Chapters can change over time as new content arrives.

| | Free | Premium |
|---|---|---|
| Chapters 1 and 2 (levels 1 to 20) | Yes | Yes |
| Chapter 3 onward | No | Yes |

See [CalcQuest Premium](../premium.md).

## Rewards on the map

| Reward | When |
|---|---|
| 50 XP and 1 gem | Every cleared level |
| Chest | At set points inside a chapter, with bonus gems |
| Trophy | At the end of each chapter |
| Achievements | Journey milestones unlock Google Play Games achievements on Android |

Clearing a Journey level also counts as a played day for your [streak](progression-and-rewards.md).

## Why the Journey mixes games

Mixing games keeps every level fresh and builds a broad set of skills: logic, arithmetic, memory and spatial reasoning in one sitting. It also means you meet games you might never have opened on your own. The thinking behind it is in [From calculator game to brain puzzles](../dev-journey/from-calculator-game-to-brain-puzzles.md).

## Playing the Journey as a guest

Guests can play the Journey with no account. Progress is kept on the phone; sign in to save it to your account and continue on another device. See [Play as a guest](guest-play.md).
