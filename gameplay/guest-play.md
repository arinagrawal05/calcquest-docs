# Playing CalcQuest as a guest

You can play CalcQuest the moment you open it, as a guest. You enter a name, and you are playing; there is no account, email or password to set up first. Guests can play every puzzle game, the Journey and the daily challenge. Signing in later keeps all your guest progress and unlocks streaks, friends, duels, leaderboards and Premium.

## What guests can do

| Feature | Guest | Signed in |
|---|---|---|
| Every puzzle game | Yes | Yes |
| The Journey | Yes | Yes |
| Daily challenge | Yes | Yes |
| XP and gems | Yes, on this phone | Yes, saved to your account |
| Streaks and streak rewards | No | Yes |
| Leaderboards and friends | No | Yes |
| Duels and invites | No | Yes |
| Premium and purchases | No | Yes |
| Progress on other devices | No | Yes |

## Signing in

Sign in with your phone number (a one-time code), Google, or Apple. A new account is created straight away from the name you gave as a guest, with no extra form, and keeps your guest progress.

If you sign in to an account that already exists, CalcQuest shows which progress will be kept before anything is replaced.

## When CalcQuest asks you to sign in

After a few finished games, CalcQuest gently suggests signing in so your progress is safe. After more games, it asks you to sign in to keep playing. You are also asked when you open something that needs an account, such as duels or the streak screen.

## Joining from a friend's link

If a friend's invite link brought you to CalcQuest, their referral code is filled in for you, and you both get gems when you join: 20 for you and 10 for them. See [Invite friends](../campaigns/invite-friends.md).

## Why guest-first

The best puzzle games let you play within seconds. Asking for an account before someone has solved a single puzzle loses players who would have loved the game. The reasoning is in [UX decisions](../design-philosophy/ux-decisions.md).
