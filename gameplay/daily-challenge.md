# The CalcQuest daily challenge

The daily challenge is a set of four puzzle games that is the same for every CalcQuest player that day. You play all four against a stopwatch, and your total time goes on the daily leaderboard. There is one attempt per day, and friends are told when you beat their time, so it works as a small shared ritual.

## How it works

1. Open the Games tab and start today's daily challenge.
2. Play the four games in turn. The stopwatch runs across all four.
3. When the fourth game is done, your total time is saved to the daily leaderboard.
4. Share your result card, or check where you stand among friends.

A new set of four games arrives every day at midnight, local time.

## The same puzzle for everyone

The four games are chosen from the date, so every player gets the same set on the same day. That makes the daily challenge something to compare and talk about: "What was your time today?"

## Beat your friends

When your time beats a friend's, CalcQuest sends them a notification so they can try to win it back. The number of these notifications is capped, so nobody is flooded.

## The daily challenge and your streak

Finishing the daily challenge counts as a played day for your streak. See [Progress and rewards](progression-and-rewards.md) and [Streak rewards](../campaigns/streak-rewards.md).

## Calculator Classic daily

[Calculator Classic](../games/calculator-classic.md) has its own daily set of five levels, separate from the four-game daily challenge.

## Common questions

**Can I replay the daily challenge?** No. One attempt per day keeps the leaderboard fair.

**Do I need an account?** Guests can play the daily challenge; the daily leaderboard and friend comparisons need a signed-in account.

**Is the daily challenge free?** Yes, for everyone.
