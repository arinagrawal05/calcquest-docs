# Play CalcQuest puzzles free on the web

Nine CalcQuest puzzles play free in any browser at [calcquest.app](https://calcquest.app), with no download and no account: Queens, Tango, KenKen, Mini Sudoku, Shikaku, Nonogram, Binairo, Minesweeper and Number Pyramid. Each has Easy, Medium and Hard boards and a tutorial. The full game, with the Journey, daily challenge, duels and 20+ games, is in the Android and iOS app.

## Web or app?

| | Web | App |
|---|---|---|
| Puzzles | 9 | 20+ |
| Account needed | No | No (guest play) |
| Journey, daily challenge, duels | No | Yes |
| Streaks, XP, gems, rewards | No | Yes |
| Offline play | No | Yes |

The web puzzles are a free taste of CalcQuest. To keep a streak and play with friends, get the app on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.calcquest.calculatorgame) or the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6757161847).

## Links that open the app

Links from CalcQuest, such as a friend's duel, an invite or a game link (for example `calcquest.app/game/queens`), open the app directly when it is installed. Without the app, they lead to the store and open the right screen after you install.
