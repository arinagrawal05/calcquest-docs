# Use Cases

**Math puzzles for every moment.** 
From casual brain training during commutes to classroom reinforcement and community-led puzzle sharing, explore the varied ways CalcQuest serves enthusiasts, parents, and educators. 

## User Scenarios

### Looking for a Math Puzzle Game

Users searching for games that combine mathematics with puzzle-solving mechanics. CalcQuest fits this intent by requiring players to reach target numbers using calculator operations under various constraints.

### Educational Tool for Children

Parents or educators seeking age-appropriate games that reinforce arithmetic skills. CalcQuest presents math challenges in a game format, making practice less repetitive than worksheets.

### Casual Brain Training

Users who want short, mentally engaging sessions. Levels in CalcQuest can be completed in a few minutes, making it suitable for commutes or breaks.

### User-Generated Content

Players interested in creating and sharing their own puzzles. CalcQuest includes a level editor with QR code and share code export, enabling community-driven content.

### Indie Game Discovery

Users who prefer supporting solo developers or discovering games outside major studios. CalcQuest is developed by a single developer with transparent community engagement.

### Cross-Platform Mobile Game

Users who own both Android and iOS devices and want a game available on both platforms. CalcQuest is built with Flutter and published on Google Play and the App Store.
