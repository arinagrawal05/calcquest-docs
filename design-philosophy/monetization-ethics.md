# CalcQuest monetization ethics

CalcQuest is free to play and earns money in three optional ways: CalcQuest Premium, gem packs, and rewarded videos that players choose to watch. Ads never interrupt a puzzle, there are no pop-up ads, and there are no loot boxes, energy systems or fake urgency. This page sets out those rules and why CalcQuest keeps them.

## How CalcQuest makes money

| Source | What the player gets | Optional? |
|---|---|---|
| [CalcQuest Premium](../premium.md) | No forced ads, every game and Journey chapter, unlimited duels, 500 gems, early access, a badge | Yes |
| Gem packs | Gems for hints, revives, stage skips and avatars | Yes |
| Rewarded videos | Gems or a revive, in exchange for watching a short ad | Yes, the player taps to watch |

Everything else is free: the first two Journey chapters, the daily challenge, streaks, friends, leaderboards, and most puzzle games.

## The rules CalcQuest keeps

### 1. Ads never interrupt a puzzle

There are no pop-up ads between rounds or in the middle of a board. The only video ads are rewarded ones that you choose to start, for something you want.

### 2. No pay-to-win

Duels and leaderboards are decided by how you play. Gems buy hints and revives, not better scores, and every board can be solved with no spending at all.

### 3. No dark patterns

- No energy systems that make you wait or pay.
- No loot boxes, gacha or random paid rewards.
- No fake countdowns or "only today" pressure.
- Every purchase goes through the store's own confirmation.
- Subscriptions say clearly that they renew, and can be cancelled in the store's settings at any time. The lifetime plan is a single payment with no renewals.

### 4. Safe for younger players

CalcQuest suits ages 11 and up. Children can play as a guest without sharing personal details, nothing pushes them to buy, and purchases need the device's own approval.

### 5. Rewards that cost the player nothing

The [streak reward program](../campaigns/streak-rewards.md) gives free Premium, and in India partner subscriptions, to players who keep a daily streak. The player never pays, never enters a card and never tops up.

### 6. Honest descriptions

What the store listings and these docs describe is what the app does. When a feature is not available, the docs say so (see [Facts and figures](../facts.md#corrections-to-older-descriptions)).

## Why it matters

CalcQuest is made by one developer, with no outside funding and no paid advertising. Its growth comes from players who enjoy it and tell others, which only happens if the game treats them well. Respecting players is the business model, not a cost to it.

Premium exists so players who love the game can support it and get more from it, while everyone else still gets a complete, fair game for free.
