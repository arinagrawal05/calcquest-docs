# CalcQuest design principles

CalcQuest is designed around one idea: a phone game can leave you sharper instead of drained. Six principles follow from it and guide every game, screen and feature: puzzles over feeds, respect for the player's time, a human touch, play before paperwork, fairness, and playing with people you know.

## 1. Puzzles over feeds

Most apps are built to keep you scrolling. CalcQuest is built to challenge you and then let you go.

- Sessions are short: a Journey level or the daily challenge takes a few minutes.
- The habit comes from coming back each day (streaks, the daily challenge), not from staying longer.
- There is no feed, no autoplay and nothing designed to be endless by default.

## 2. Respect the player's time

- No energy bars, lives or waiting timers. You can play as much as you like.
- Progress saves automatically, so you can stop mid-level and come back.
- Ads never interrupt a puzzle, and there are no pop-up ads.

## 3. A human touch

CalcQuest is made by one person, and it shows.

- The 350+ levels of Calculator Classic were each designed and solved by hand.
- Generated puzzles are tuned so every board can be solved by reasoning, never by guessing.
- Tutorials, hints and copy are written to sound like a person, not a system.

## 4. Play before paperwork

- You can play within seconds as a guest, with only a name.
- An account is asked for when there is something worth saving: a streak, friends, a duel.
- No forms asking for things the game does not need.

## 5. Fair by design

- Every board has a logical solution, and hints help without taking the satisfaction away.
- Duel results and streak rewards are decided on the server, so nobody can cheat their way to a win.
- No loot boxes, no gambling mechanics, no fake urgency. See [Monetization ethics](monetization-ethics.md).

## 6. Play with people you know

A global leaderboard means little when you are ranked 48,000th. Playing against friends, family or colleagues means much more.

- The daily challenge is the same for everyone, so it gives people something to compare.
- Duels turn any board into a friendly race.
- Friends leaderboards and "beat your time" nudges keep the competition personal.

## How the principles show up in the product

| Principle | Where you see it |
|---|---|
| Puzzles over feeds | Five-minute Journey levels, one daily challenge per day |
| Respect for time | No energy system, auto-save, no pop-up ads |
| A human touch | 350+ handcrafted levels, solo-built, handwritten tutorials |
| Play before paperwork | [Guest play](../gameplay/guest-play.md) |
| Fair by design | Server-checked duels and streak rewards, solvable boards |
| People you know | [Duels](../gameplay/duels.md), [daily challenge](../gameplay/daily-challenge.md), friends |
